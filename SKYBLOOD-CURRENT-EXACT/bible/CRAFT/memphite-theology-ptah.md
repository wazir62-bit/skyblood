## What the document is arguing

The Memphite Theology is not a children’s story of Ptah. It is a **claim of priority**.

Heliopolis already had Atum: the world from a body. Thebes had Amun. Memphis answers: **Ptah is before that.** The other gods happen *in* him.

Shabaka’s own frame is the other claim: *His majesty found it worm-eaten, copied it anew, better than before, so that his name might endure in the house of his father Ptah-South-of-his-Wall.*

A king **files a restored copy** so a name will last. That is the stone’s first plot. SKYBLOOD already parodies it: they file a washed 1996 print and call it a record. They are not Shabaka. They are the worm.

## Ptah’s offices in the text

**1. Container.**  
“The gods who came into being in Ptah.” Ptah-Nun, father who made Atum. Ptah-Naunet, mother who bore Atum. Atum is not denied. Atum is **nested**. Heart and tongue take the shape of Atum.

**2. Heart and tongue of the Nine.**  
“Ptah-the-Great is heart and tongue of the Ennead.”  
Horus takes shape in the heart. Thoth takes shape in the tongue.  
Sight, hearing, breath go to the heart. The heart devises. The tongue repeats what the heart thought. Command follows conception.

**3. Mouth instead of body.**  
Atum’s Ennead: semen and fingers.  
Ptah’s Ennead: **teeth and lips** in the mouth that pronounced the name of every thing. Shu and Tefnut come from that pronunciation.

Same world. Different tool.

**4. Life to gods and kas.**  
He gave life to all the gods and their *kas* through this heart and this tongue. Not a blast. A **fed name**.

**5. In every body, in every mouth.**  
Heart in every body; tongue in every mouth — gods, people, cattle, whatever lives. Thinking what it wishes. Commanding what it wishes. This is the dangerous sentence. In SKYBLOOD, **command is not granted to Elias.** PB01 wins. The theology may describe a god. It does not unlock weather.

**6. South-of-his-Wall. Tatenen.**  
Local Memphite earth and enclosure. Unification of Two Lands. Osiris drowned, brought here, buried. Memphis as a **place a body is carried to**, not only a place a word is spoken.

1. Something takes shape in the **heart**.  
2. It takes shape on the **tongue**.  
3. The mouth **pronounces the name**.  
4. Things and *kas* live.

Skip a step and you get SKYBLOOD’s villains:

| Skipped | On the page |
|---|---|

## What Ptah is *for* in this theology

Not “craftsman mascot.”

He is the argument that **speech is prior to substance**, and that a restored **copy** can keep a name alive.

SKYBLOOD agrees with the first half only when the heart has gone first.  
SKYBLOOD **refuses** the second half when the copy is theirs. Issue #7: you took paper; you didn’t take her. A copy does not keep a *ren*. A kitchen does.

Two records. Theirs: the stone as they would file it. His: clock-in, unsigned line, a plate in the sink.
