# Scale symbolism

**Status: SUGGESTION.** Motion of the pans is already story canon.

The scale is **distant machinery**, not decorative Egyptian iconography. It is the office of **Weigh**.

| Issue | Motion | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| #1 p.32 | Does not move | Circuit not begun |
| #2 | Ticks **one degree** | Mae addressed + received |
| #3 | Hangs mid-motion | Weighing in progress (status) |
| #4 | Tries to drop, **catches** | Stolen close refused |

A second tick = a second completed name or a true Close. Contractor’s clipboard is a **counterfeit scale**. Ink that beads is the real machine overruling the fake.

Elias does not grab the pans. He is Way-Opener, not weigher. Close is still empty.
