# Elias — reaction grammar

**Status: SUGGESTION.** Must yield to SB-PB01 if they fight.  
Distilled from the locked Issue #1 script and published #2–#3.

His uniqueness is not a power pose. It is how he **reacts**.

---

## One line

He treats the sky the way he treats a mis-scan: notice early, fix what he can, do not make a speech, and do not let the next person get written into the gap.

---

## Eight faces (draw these, not “stoic”)

1. Work-economy (label, vest, manifest)
2. Listening-with-skin (*Hold’s coming*)
3. Smile that doesn’t arrive
4. Believed = still (worse at being believed than mocked)
5. Hand moving before he decides (**M—**)
6. The **turn-back** (Dez)
7. On his back, understood, silent
8. Look away from the twitch

---

## Graded speech

| Safe | Edge | Refused | Chosen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Labels, minutes, *That’s the job*, *I clocked in* | *There was a building. I photographed it.* | Way-Opener; cosmology | *Mae.* Then Mother finishes the name |
| | | | *I’m not going to help you write the next line.* |

If he explains heaven, he is out of character. If he says the title, that is an **event**.

---

## Body rules

- Work first, myth second.
- Perception hits the body, not the mouth.
- Turns his **body** toward people, his **face** away from the sky.
- After recognition, he does not act recognized.
- Photographs instead of insisting.

He never: commands weather, drafts Sister, knocks through Mother’s door, fills the crew gap, bleeds gold, enjoys the sight.
