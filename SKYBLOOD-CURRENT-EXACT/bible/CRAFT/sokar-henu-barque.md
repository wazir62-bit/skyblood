# Sokar and the Henu barque

**Status: SUGGESTION.** Memphis geography makes this hotter than the Neshmet. Not a plot lock. No hawk on the vest.

**Sokar** (Seker): falcon god of the **Memphite** necropolis (Saqqara recalls his name). Patron of **craftsmen** who build tombs and ritual objects — workers, not kings. Native to the realm of the dead; Osiris *dies into* him. Festival: Khoiak 26, **following Sokar when he goes round the Walls** of Memphis.

**Henu / Hennu:** his barque. High prow (oryx), funerary chest. Often a **sledge** for sand as much as a boat. Carried on priests’ shoulders in a circuit. Not the Neshmet (Osiris, Nile, Abydos, leave-and-return). The Henu **goes around the city that is already the graveyard**.

SKYBLOOD rhymes: Elias works **Memphis cargo**. Night sort as a circuit of walls. GSE as sledge. Craftsman-patron, not pharaoh. Do not: Sokar-costume, oryx prow, “he is Sokar,” boarding the Henu.

**One line:** Neshmet is the dead king’s river-homecoming. Henu is Memphis taking its own dead for a walk around the walls. Elias is already inside that walk. He does not get a second boat.
