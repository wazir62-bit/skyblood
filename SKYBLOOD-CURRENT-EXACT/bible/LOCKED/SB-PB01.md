## Core rule

**Elias perceives stress in the living boundary connecting Earth, the celestial stations, and the realm of the dead. It first presents as weather behaving incorrectly, then resolves into threshold geometry.**

“Join” may be informal production language. **Threshold** is the governing term.

He is sensitive **before** instruments or coworkers. He cannot summon recognition. Recognition is not a costume grant or a conscious power activation.

---

## Ranked sense stack

1. **Physical atmospheric sensitivity**
2. **Atmospheric empathy**
3. **Visual threshold sight**
4. **Reciprocal sight**

Visual is not always second. He can feel the boundary without seeing it.

### Physical (begins here)

Pressure, inner-ear disturbance, static, temperature change, metallic taste, directional awareness.

### Atmospheric empathy

Does **not** mean he believes the storm has ordinary human emotions.

He senses **intention, pressure, imbalance, recognition, and response** within a living atmospheric boundary.

His emotions can provoke an **involuntary** atmospheric response. He **cannot command** it deliberately in Issues #1–3.

*(Issue #4 is the first book after that ban. The ban ending is not a level-up. Recommendation: still no command.)*

### Visual threshold sight

Threshold geometry hidden inside ordinary weather. Cloud may become too ordered. Temple **resolves inside cloud** — sky-scale, not a building on the field.

### Reciprocal sight

When Elias perceives deeply enough, something on the other side can perceive him.

That is where **Way-Opener** belongs dramatically. He may hear it. He does not repeat it. He cannot use it.

## Speech vs address

**Ordinary description does not open a threshold.**

Exposure intensifies when speech becomes an **address**:

- a true name
- a recognized title
- a deliberate invocation
- testimony that another person **genuinely receives**

This preserves **SB-L17:** Address → Receive → Weigh → Close.

He stays quiet because disbelief wounded him **and** because some forms of address are dangerous — not because every sentence mechanically tears reality.

## Gold

- Gold is an **event of recognition-light**, not wardrobe, not a body state.
- Must use **existing** light, water, metal, paint, glass, or reflective fabric.
- No gold eyes, skin, veins, blood, beams, weapons, jewelry symbolism, or designed emblem.
- Gold cannot activate merely because Elias becomes emotional.
- Issue #1: Page 24 is the clearest gold event.
- Later canon may allow brief **gold-black guardian markings** — they do **not** appear in Issues #1–3 and need a later transformation lock.
