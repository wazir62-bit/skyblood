## One rule

Issue #1 clothes are **facility work clothes plus a reflective safety vest**.

Visual, not a costume. If a garment reads as designed emblem, gold trim, cape, or suit, the panel is wrong.

**Gold is an event of recognition-light, not part of Elias’s wardrobe.**

## Vest geometry (permanent H-wrap)

Reject “and/or X/chevron.” One arrangement only:

- Fluorescent yellow-green / lime **mesh** vest (Class 2)
- Two-inch silver retroreflective tape
- **Two vertical front bands running over the shoulders**
- **One horizontal band around the torso**
- No custom X, M, wing, scale, or chevron sewn in

Heroic geometry emerges only when ordinary bands align with **body + apron markings**. Found, not designed.

Bands are **silver-gray**, never painted gold as fabric.

---

## Color

- Vest: lime + silver-gray bands
- Shirt / jacket / pants / boots: charcoal, navy, dark work neutrals
- Gold: **not a fabric color**

Employer marks, if any, are workplace — not a superhero logo. Do not invent a sky-symbol as the company mark. Do not costume him in Memphis merch as identity.
