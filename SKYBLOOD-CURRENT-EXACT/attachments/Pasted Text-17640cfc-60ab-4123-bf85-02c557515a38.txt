Yes. If you want RJ to eventually feel like a real superhero with physical powers, he needs more than perception. We can give him an elemental/physical domain while keeping the Anubis connection coherent.

I would choose EARTH + GRAVITATIONAL WEIGHT, not tides.

Tides belong more naturally to lunar/gravitational mythology. Anubis gives us a cleaner path through earth, burial, weight, passage, and the scale.

RJ's power set

Power	What RJ can do	Limit

Geokinesis	Move, raise, split, compress and reshape soil/stone	Not metal/buildings indiscriminately
Ground Sense	Feel movement/contact through connected earth	Doesn't work well when isolated from ground
Weight Control	Increase/decrease effective weight of a person/object	Not full gravity control
True Weight	Sense when identity/record/body doesn't properly balance	Not mind-reading
Threshold Sense	Detect improper crossings/boundaries	Doesn't automatically identify culprit
Passage	Later: permit rightful passage across certain resistant boundaries	High-level ability; earned later


Now he can fight.

A vehicle comes at him?

RJ plants his hand:

WHUM.

Road rises and catches the axle.

Someone stronger attacks Elias?

RJ increases that person's effective weight until their knees hit concrete.

Building collapsing?

Elias physically holds the structure.

RJ drives broken supports into the earth and stabilizes the foundation.

That's a great uncle/nephew superhero combination because their powers complement rather than duplicate.

But weight control needs a strict definition

Don't give RJ generic gravity manipulation. That gets enormous very quickly—flight, black holes, crushing people, orbital effects, etc.

His power should be:

> RJ can alter how strongly something is being claimed by the ground.



That's much more SKYBLOOD.

Increase the claim → object becomes extraordinarily difficult to lift/move.

Reduce the claim → object becomes lighter/easier to move.

He isn't controlling gravity across the universe.

He's changing the relationship between a thing and the earth beneath it.

And that connects beautifully to Anubis and the scale:

physical weight → moral/mythological weight → rightful place.

What about tides?

I'd save water/tides for somebody else.

If RJ gets:

earth + gravity + tides + death + passage + truth detection,

he becomes overloaded immediately.

Earth gives him plenty of visual spectacle:

ground cracking, stone walls, raised pavement, localized tremors, sand movement, anchoring objects, sensing footsteps.

And we can establish a major limitation:

> RJ is strongest when physically connected to natural ground.



On asphalt/concrete: good.

Inside a high-rise: weaker.

Airplane: substantially weaker.

Over open water: serious disadvantage.

That creates actual fight strategy.

His eventual signature move

Not an earthquake.

Too broad and dangerous.

I would give him THE WEIGHING.

RJ puts his palm down.

The ground beneath one target depresses.

Not because RJ blasts them.

Because suddenly their weight becomes undeniable.

Cracks spread beneath their feet.

They cannot move.

RJ:

> “Stay.”



No glowing eyes.

No gold.

No Egyptian energy beam.

Just the earth accepting their weight.

That could look incredible in a comic.

So my recommendation for RJ is:

Primary: Earth manipulation.
Secondary: weight/ground relationship.
Innate: True Weight + Threshold Sense.
Later mythic ability: Passage.
No tides. No generic gravity. No Elias-copy strength.

That gives RJ enough physical power to eventually stand beside Elias in a superhero fight while remaining unmistakably Anubis-connected rather than SKYBLOOD Jr.
