This is substantially tighter.

The biggest improvement is that we stopped answering our own mystery

Removing:

> “Not where. When.”



was the right decision.

Now the double says:

> “I don't know how to say it without making it smaller.”



That's better SKYBLOOD dialogue because it suggests the problem may involve chronology without reducing it to time travel.

And then:

> ELIAS: Can't?
DOUBLE: Won't.



That's character progression. Earlier, there were things the double genuinely couldn't say. Here he makes his own decision not to answer.

He's acquiring agency at the same time Elias is beginning to recognize him as a person.

The blood sequence is much stronger: 10/10

Elias answers:

Excellent.

FIRST SKY reacted because it was blood.

Because it was his blood.

Because the double bled at 2417.

Because the blood crossed some condition.

Because trying to verbalize Grandfather's rules caused the bleeding.

MOTHER: No. He remembers something I didn't witness.



Keep that.
