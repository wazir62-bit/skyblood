TO BE CONTINUED.Review: 9.9/10

This issue pays a major historical mystery without collapsing the larger mythology.

The critical reveal is:

> “I didn’t choose you. I chose the side I was standing on.”



Thomas did make a choice—but not a conscious sacrifice of Abel. He made a boundary decision under pressure and believed that was categorically different from choosing who survived.

That is morally more interesting. Thomas can be brave, selfish, scared, loving, and wrong at the same time.

The other major payoff is Paper:

> SURVIVING RECORD COUNT: 1
SURVIVING BODY COUNT: 2



That is excellent because it finally separates record survival from bodily survival. Abel was never literally erased from existence. His body survived while his record did not.

That makes CLOSED much more precise without over-explaining the full mechanism.

And the ending introduces the Thomas-faced figure without saying “Grandfather is alive.” In fact Abel immediately rejects that conclusion:

> “That’s not Thomas.”



So the next issue has a concrete job: determine whether the figure is another return, a retained record given presentation, a boundary echo, or something else entirely.

Overall: 9.9/10.
