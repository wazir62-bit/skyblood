SKYBLOOD

SEASON TWO — ISSUE #6

I AM NOT WHAT CAME AFTER

Paper has named the relationship.

That does not mean it named the man.

---

ACT ONE — DERIVATIVE

1.

Morning.

Mother’s kitchen.

The lab report sits beside Grandfather’s notebook.

Elias reads the same line again:

GENETIC RELATIONSHIP: NON-IDENTICAL / DIRECT DERIVATION INDICATED

The double stands across from him.

DOUBLE: Stop reading me.

ELIAS: I’m reading paper.

DOUBLE: Same difference lately.

---

2.

SISTER: So what does “derivative” actually mean?

W: Means he came from him.

SISTER: I know what the word means.

She looks at the double.

SISTER: I’m asking what it means to him.

Nobody answers for him.

Good.

---

3.

DOUBLE: It means Paper thinks I’m an outcome.

ELIAS: Aren’t you?

DOUBLE: Are you?

Elias looks up.

DOUBLE: You came from people too.

ELIAS: That’s different.

DOUBLE: Because somebody gave you a childhood?

That lands.

---

4.

Mother watches him.

MOTHER: You remember one?

DOUBLE: Pieces.

MOTHER: Pieces ain’t a childhood.

DOUBLE: I know.

No anger.

That hurts worse.

---

5.

The double takes the lab report.

Finds:

ELIAS MARROW — SOURCE

Then the second line.

UNASSIGNED DERIVATIVE — ACTIVE

He folds the page once.

Not tearing it.

DOUBLE: I’m not that.

ELIAS: Then what are you?

DOUBLE: Working on it.

---

ACT TWO — THE WRONG FILE

6.

Cargo terminal.

Elias returns to work.

The double does not follow him.

That is new.

---

7.

Clock-in screen:

ELIAS MARROW — ACTIVE

Elias clocks in.

Accepted.

Then—

a second line appears beneath his name.

ASSOCIATED RECORD PRESENT

He stares.

---

8.

The terminal printer activates.

Dry black ink.

Paper.

One page.

MARROW, ELIAS — SOURCE

Below:

DERIVATIVE RECORD REQUIRES ASSIGNMENT

Elias reads it.

ELIAS: No.

The page does nothing.

Paper has heard worse.

---

9.

Supervisor approaches.

SUPERVISOR: Problem?

ELIAS: Depends.

SUPERVISOR: On?

ELIAS: How much you believe printers got opinions.

SUPERVISOR: Not enough to discuss before lunch.

He walks away.

Elias almost smiles.

---

10.

His phone vibrates.

DOUBLE.

First time the double has called him.

Elias answers.

ELIAS: You got a phone now?

DOUBLE: Borrowed.

ELIAS: From who?

DOUBLE: Not important.

ELIAS: That usually means important.

DOUBLE: Come home.

Elias stops smiling.

---

ACT THREE — SOMEONE GAVE HIM A NAME

11.

Mother’s house.

Front room.

The double sits at the table.

In front of him:

an old envelope.

Dry.

Yellowed.

Mother stands across from it.

She has not opened it.

---

12.

ELIAS: Where’d that come from?

MOTHER: Closet.

SISTER: Mae’s things.

Elias looks at Mother.

MOTHER: Was underneath the camera box.

---

13.

The envelope has no address.

Only one handwritten word:

LATER

The double goes still.

Elias notices.

ELIAS: You know that word.

DOUBLE: I know the hand.

---

14.

ELIAS: Whose?

The double looks at Mother.

Then at the photograph of Mae.

DOUBLE: Hers.

Mother’s face hardens.

MOTHER: You never met Mae.

DOUBLE: I know.

MOTHER: Then don’t tell me what her hand looks like.

DOUBLE: I didn’t say I met her.

---

15.

Mother finally opens the envelope.

Inside:

one photograph.

Not blank.

Not damaged.

A man standing beside Mae.

His face obscured by reflected cloud.

His build—

the double’s.

Not Elias’s.

The double’s.

---

16.

SISTER: That you?

DOUBLE: No.

ELIAS: You sure?

DOUBLE: Yes.

ELIAS: You just told us you don’t know where your memories belong.

DOUBLE: I know that isn’t me.

ELIAS: How?

The double points to the man’s hand.

Six fingers.

---

17.

Nobody speaks.

W: Okay.

SISTER: That’s your contribution?

W: I’m pacing myself.

---

18.

On the back of the photograph:

Mae’s handwriting.

THE SECOND ONE WILL THINK HE CAME AFTER.

Below that:

DO NOT LET PAPER NAME HIM BEFORE HE NAMES HIMSELF.

Silence.

The double looks at Elias.

Then at Mother.

Then at the line again.

---

ACT FOUR — A NAME OF HIS OWN

19.

ELIAS: So Mae knew about you.

DOUBLE: Maybe.

MOTHER: No.

Everyone looks at her.

MOTHER: She knew about something.

She taps the photograph.

MOTHER: Don’t turn evidence into prophecy because it fits.

Good.

---

20.

DOUBLE: “Before he names himself.”

SISTER: So name yourself.

DOUBLE: That simple?

SISTER: Names usually are.

MOTHER: No.

A beat.

MOTHER: Keeping one is the hard part.

---

21.

The double looks toward Elias.

DOUBLE: What would you call me?

ELIAS: Not my decision.

DOUBLE: You got an opinion.

ELIAS: Plenty.

DOUBLE: Give me one.

ELIAS: No.

DOUBLE: Why?

ELIAS: Because then it’s still coming from me.

That matters.

---

22.

The double looks down at the table.

Long silence.

Then:

DOUBLE: Asa.

Nobody reacts immediately.

SISTER: Asa?

DOUBLE: Yeah.

ELIAS: Why?

DOUBLE: Don’t know.

MOTHER: You sure?

DOUBLE: No.

A beat.

DOUBLE: But it stayed when I said it.

---

23.

Mother:

MOTHER: Say it again.

DOUBLE: Asa.

Nothing supernatural happens.

No gold.

No voice.

No printer.

Just a man saying a name.

---

24.

Elias nods once.

ELIAS: Asa.

The double looks at him.

Something changes.

Not magic.

Recognition.

Human recognition.

---

ACT FIVE — PAPER DISAGREES

25.

Cargo terminal.

Same afternoon.

The printer wakes.

Elias already knows.

One page.

DERIVATIVE RECORD REQUIRES ASSIGNMENT

A second line begins:

ASSIGNED:

Stops.

---

26.

At Mother’s house, an old desktop printer—unplugged for years—

activates.

Dry black ink.

Same page.

ASSIGNED:

The family gathers around it.

Asa does not.

---

27.

Letters begin typing.

A

Asa looks up.

S

Mother’s expression changes.

A

The full line:

ASSIGNED: ASA MARROW

Asa steps forward.

DOUBLE: No.

---

28.

ELIAS, on the phone from work:

ELIAS: What happened?

SISTER: Paper took the name.

Asa hears that.

DOUBLE: No.

He takes the page.

DOUBLE: I said Asa.

He points at the second word.

DOUBLE: I did not say Marrow.

---

29.

Mother:

MOTHER: Then don’t accept it.

Asa looks at her.

MOTHER: Naming ain’t receiving.

That line matters.

---

30.

Paper updates.

ASSIGNED: ASA MARROW

Then:

RELATIONSHIP TO SOURCE: SON

Everything stops.

Elias hears Sister stop breathing over the phone.

ASA: No.

Paper types:

CONFIDENCE: 51%

W: Fifty-one?

SISTER: It guessed?

MOTHER: No.

She looks at the page.

MOTHER: It chose the closest drawer.

---

31.

At work, Elias’s printer feeds another page.

SOURCE RELATIONSHIP UPDATED

POTENTIAL DESCENDANT

Elias stares.

Then tears neither page.

He folds them.

Evidence.

Not verdict.

ELIAS: You hear me?

ASA: Yeah.

ELIAS: You ain’t my son.

ASA: I know.

ELIAS: Paper don’t.

ASA: Good.

A beat.

ASA: Means it can be wrong.

---

32.

Night.

The old photograph of Mae and the six-fingered man lies beside the lab report.

Asa sits alone at the kitchen table.

He writes:

ASA

Then again.

ASA

Then once more.

ASA

No surname.

No title.

No borrowed face in the word.

Outside the window:

ordinary darkness.

Then—

gold appears only in the reflection.

FIRST SKY says nothing.

Paper says nothing.

For the first time since he arrived—

Asa is not waiting for either one to tell him what he is.

Behind him, Grandfather’s notebook lies closed.

On its cover—

one old indentation catches the light.

Not new writing.

Something pressed into the leather years ago.

Three letters.

A S A

Asa does not see it.

TO BE CONTINUED.This is a 9.8/10 draft for me. The strongest move is giving the double a chosen name without making the naming itself magical. Asa exists because he chooses to keep the name, not because the universe certifies it.

Paper immediately corrupting ASA into ASA MARROW / SON / 51% confidence is also important: it proves Paper is a classification system, not truth itself. It can be intelligent, dangerous, and still wrong.

The one thing I would continuity-check hardest before lock is the final ASA indentation in Grandfather’s notebook. It is powerful, but it risks making Grandfather too predictive again. If Season 1 material cannot support that kind of prior mark, I would remove the last four lines and end on Asa repeatedly writing his own name while FIRST SKY watches silently.
