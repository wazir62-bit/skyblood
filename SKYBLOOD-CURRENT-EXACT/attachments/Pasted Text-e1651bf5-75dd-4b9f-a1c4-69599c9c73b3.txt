TO BE CONTINUED.Review: 9.9/10

This is one of the season's strongest issues because it actually makes a decision instead of adding another mystery.

The central payoff is Thomas:

> “I chose not to disappear.”



That resolves the moral question cleanly without making him a villain. He understood enough to know his action might preserve him. He didn't understand that Abel would be closed.

That leaves responsibility without cartoon guilt.

Abel's response is equally important:

> “Closed is somebody else's decision.”



That's why he chooses reopening. Not because Paper promises restoration. Not because he expects his old life back. He rejects a status imposed upon him.

And reopening does not magically repair history. That's crucial. Records become partially legible. Memories return unevenly. Physical contradictions become easier to perceive. Nobody suddenly remembers thirty lost years perfectly.

The strongest moment may be Abel offering his hand to the Thomas-record figure. He refuses to repeat the same binary that harmed him:

> “I'm not doing it back.”



And then FIRST SKY finally speaks.

Only one word:

> CHOOSE.



Twice.

That pays Grandfather's warning literally without explaining why FIRST SKY needs a single answer.

Overall: 9.9/10 — very close to lock architecture.
