W has not disappeared. He has been active throughout Season 3, but #8 pushed him too far into the background.

His recent track is: he witnessed Elias's growing exposure after the warehouse rescue; helped Elias recognize that the viral footage was dangerous; was present through the T-17 sabotage and B-6 failure; helped Elias investigate the missing service tags; witnessed the duplicate-Elias access problem; and in #6 he was physically on the ramp helping manage the false-Elias incident. In #7–#8, though, he became mostly a phone/contact for Elias. That's where we're starting to drift with him.

W should remain one of Elias's primary anchors because he occupies a position nobody else does:

Mother = family/ethics.
Sister = documentation/family witness.
RJ = Anubis-connected family/observation.
W = work/ramp/ordinary-world brotherhood.

And W has another crucial function: he knew Elias before Elias became interesting. That makes him more valuable as Elias becomes publicly visible.

So I would correct #8 slightly: when Elias and RJ discover from the shoes that false-Elias didn't approach Gate C from the lot, Elias calls W—but W should already be investigating from inside. Elias shouldn't constantly give W instructions.

Something like:

> ELIAS: He didn't come through the lot.
W: I know.
ELIAS: How?
W: Because I been working while you playing detective.



Then W reveals he's already checked the service corridor.

That restores his competence.

More importantly, #9 should give W substantial page time. Not as Elias's assistant. He's the man still inside the workplace while Elias has lost access. That means W currently has something Elias doesn't:

access.

The false Elias can fool a badge reader.

He can copy Elias's photograph.

He can put on lime.

But W knows the actual man.

That makes W potentially one of the hardest people in the entire story to fool.

So W hasn't gone anywhere—but you caught another continuity pressure early. We need to pull him back toward the center starting next issue rather than letting RJ replace his narrative function. RJ adds a new relationship; he does not replace W.
