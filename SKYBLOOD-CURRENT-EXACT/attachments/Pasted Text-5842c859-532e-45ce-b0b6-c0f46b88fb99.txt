**SB-WD01 is approved in principle but not ready to lock.** Three continuity choices remain too open for a character-consistency board.

## Required marks

### 1. Vest geometry must be exact

Reject:

> “horizontal and/or X/chevron as real vests have”

“And/or” allows Elias’s most important garment to change between panels. Grok must select one permanent tape arrangement for Issue #1 and show front and back.

My production decision:

* Fluorescent yellow-green mesh vest
* Two-inch silver retroreflective tape
* Two vertical front bands running over the shoulders
* One horizontal band around the torso
* No custom X, M, wing, scale, or chevron sewn into it
* The heroic geometry emerges only when the ordinary bands align with Elias’s body and apron markings

This keeps the vest realistic and prevents the emblem from being built into the clothing.

### 2. Default clothing cannot remain optional

“Long- or short-sleeve,” “jacket optional,” and “under or with the vest” permit wardrobe drift inside one continuous shift.

Lock one shift outfit:

* Dark charcoal long-sleeve work shirt
* Dark navy weather-resistant work jacket
* Vest worn over the jacket during every apron sequence
* Dark work pants
* Plain black safety-toe work boots
* No outfit changes between the hold, Dez rescue, temple, strike, and Page 24

The jacket can come off indoors before the storm sequence, but once Elias enters the apron sequence, continuity must remain exact.

### 3. Safety props need fixed placement

“If the hub uses them” is too loose.

Lock:

* Plain employee badge clipped below the vest’s left side—not centered on his chest
* Small work radio on his belt with a shoulder microphone
* No magical lighting or symbolic framing
* No helmet
* Hearing protection may appear during normal operations but must be removed or displaced before the temple spread so it does not obscure Ceril’s face

## Approved without changes

* Silver reflective bands—not gold trim
* T pendant, chain, watch, club clothes, tattoos, and guardian markings remain off Issue #1
* Page 24 keeps the same soaked work outfit
* Elias remains on his back
* No costume change during the rescue or strike
* Coworkers wear the same class of PPE without matching Elias exactly
* Gold remains reflected recognition-light, never fabric color
* No employer or Memphis branding used as superhero identity

**Ruling:** Grok should issue **SB-WD01 Rev. 2** with those three exact locks. Once corrected, the wardrobe board can be permanently approved.
