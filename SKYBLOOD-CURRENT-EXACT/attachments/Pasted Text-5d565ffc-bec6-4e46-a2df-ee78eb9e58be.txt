SKYBLOOD

SEASON TWO — ISSUE #7

THE NAME HE KEPT

Paper gave him a surname.

He did not take it.

---

ACT ONE — ASA

1.

Morning.

Mother’s kitchen.

A sheet of notebook paper sits on the table.

Three handwritten lines:

ASA

ASA

ASA

No surname.

---

2.

Asa is asleep in a chair.

Not Elias’s chair.

Not Grandfather’s.

A folding chair brought in from another room.

Small distinction.

Important.

---

3.

Sister enters.

Sees the paper.

Sees Asa.

SISTER: You stay here all night?

ASA: Mostly.

SISTER: “Mostly” where?

ASA: Here.

SISTER: Then that just means yes.

ASA: Learning that.

---

4.

Mother enters.

Sets down coffee.

One cup in front of Asa.

No ceremony.

ASA: That mine?

MOTHER: You sitting there.

ASA takes it.

First ordinary thing Mother has given him.

---

5.

Elias enters.

Work vest.

Bandage gone now.

He sees Asa’s paper.

ELIAS: Still like it?

ASA: Yeah.

ELIAS: Good.

ASA: You?

ELIAS: Ain’t mine to like.

Asa looks at him.

That is the right answer.

---

ACT TWO — THE RECORD DOESN’T AGREE

6.

Cargo terminal.

Elias clocks in.

Screen:

ELIAS MARROW — ACTIVE

Below:

ASSOCIATED RECORD — ASA MARROW

Elias stares.

---

7.

He taps the second line.

The system opens a profile.

No photo.

No employee number.

No department.

Just:

RELATION: POTENTIAL DESCENDANT

CONFIDENCE: 51%

---

8.

ELIAS: Fifty-one ain’t enough to ruin nobody’s life.

COWORKER: What?

ELIAS: Nothing.

COWORKER: You talk to machines too much now.

ELIAS: They started it.

---

9.

Printer wakes.

Dry black ink.

Paper.

ASA MARROW — PROVISIONAL

SOURCE: ELIAS MARROW

STATUS: UNPLACED

Elias reads the last word twice.

---

10.

At Mother’s house—

the unplugged printer starts.

Same page.

Asa watches from across the room.

He does not approach.

---

11.

SISTER: You gonna read it?

ASA: No.

SISTER: Why?

ASA: Already know what it thinks.

MOTHER: Thinking ain’t the same as knowing.

Asa looks at her.

MOTHER: That goes for Paper too.

---

ACT THREE — THE FIRST PLACE THAT CALLS HIM ASA

12.

Afternoon.

Neighborhood convenience store.

Elias and Asa enter together.

Two men with the same face.

Different clothes now.

Asa has chosen his own jacket.

Nothing symbolic.

Just his.

---

13.

CASHIER looks between them.

CASHIER: Y’all brothers?

ELIAS: No.

ASA: No.

Again at the same time.

CASHIER: Cousins?

ELIAS: No.

ASA: Maybe.

Elias looks at him.

ASA: We don’t know.

CASHIER: Family complicated.

ELIAS: You have no idea.

---

14.

At checkout:

CASHIER: Name?

ASA: Asa.

CASHIER: For the order.

ASA: Asa.

The cashier writes:

ASA

on a paper bag.

Nothing happens.

No gold.

No Paper.

No FIRST SKY.

---

15.

Asa stares at the bag.

ELIAS: What?

ASA: Nothing.

ELIAS: That ain’t nothing.

ASA: First time somebody wrote it because I told them to.

Elias understands.

---

16.

Outside.

Asa carries the bag.

Not like evidence.

Like lunch.

ASA: Feels different.

ELIAS: Should.

ASA: Why?

ELIAS: Because nobody was trying to decide what you were for.

---

ACT FOUR — THE MAN IN THE PHOTOGRAPH

17.

Mother’s house.

The old photograph of Mae and the six-fingered man is on the table.

Asa returns.

Stops when he sees it.

---

18.

MOTHER: I found another one.

Second photograph.

Same man.

Same obscured face.

This time standing beside Grandfather.

Years later.

---

19.

ELIAS: Same guy?

MOTHER: Same hand.

Six fingers visible.

ASA: Same coat.

Mother looks at him.

MOTHER: You remember it?

ASA: No.

A beat.

ASA: I know it.

---

20.

ELIAS: Difference?

ASA: Memory feels like something behind me.

He points to the coat.

ASA: That feels like something waiting in front.

No one likes that.

---

21.

On the back of the photograph:

Grandfather’s handwriting.

Not prophecy.

A date from decades ago.

And one line:

HE WOULD NOT GIVE ME HIS NAME.

---

22.

SISTER: Six fingers got a name?

MOTHER: Apparently not one he gave Granddad.

ELIAS: Mae knew him.

MOTHER: Maybe.

ASA looks at the first photograph.

At Mae.

ASA: Or she knew what came before him.

---

ACT FIVE — UNPLACED

23.

That night.

Paper activates.

At work.

At Mother’s house.

At the lab.

Three printers.

Same line.

ASA MARROW — PROVISIONAL

Then:

STATUS: UNPLACED

---

24.

At Mother’s house.

Asa finally walks to the printer.

Takes the page.

SISTER: Thought you wasn’t reading those.

ASA: Changed my mind.

---

25.

He crosses out:

MARROW

Leaves:

ASA

Paper immediately reprints:

ASA MARROW

Asa crosses out MARROW again.

Another page.

Again.

Again.

Again.

---

26.

ELIAS, watching:

ELIAS: You gonna keep doing that?

ASA: Yeah.

ELIAS: Paper got more paper than you got patience.

ASA: We’ll see.

---

27.

Mother takes one of the crossed-out sheets.

MOTHER: Stop.

Asa looks at her.

MOTHER: Don’t argue with a filing cabinet.

ASA: It keeps naming me.

MOTHER: Then live louder than the file.

That stops him.

---

28.

Asa looks at the pile.

Then folds every page.

Not destroyed.

Not accepted.

Stored.

Evidence of disagreement.

---

29.

At 2417.

The room behind the wall.

Empty.

The voice claiming Grandfather has not spoken again.

On the floor:

one new footprint.

Six toes.

---

30.

Elias and W find it.

W: That ain’t yours.

ELIAS: No.

W: Asa?

ELIAS: Five toes.

W looks into the dark opening.

W: You think that’s the man in the picture?

ELIAS: I think we finally got something that ain't wearing my face.

---

31.

Behind them:

ASA: Don’t go in.

Elias turns.

ASA stands at the fire line.

ELIAS: You followed me?

ASA: No.

ELIAS: Then how’d you know?

Asa looks at the six-toed print.

His face changes.

ASA: Because I remember that foot.

---

32.

Silence.

ELIAS: From where?

Asa kneels.

Does not touch the print.

ASA: The first place I woke up.

ELIAS: Behind me?

ASA: No.

He looks toward the room behind the wall.

ASA: Before that.

A beat.

ASA: I was looking at him.

From inside the darkness—

six fingers curl slowly around the edge of the opening.

Not Elias.

Not Asa.

A different hand.

Human-shaped.

Wrong by one.

Then a voice:

VOICE: Asa.

Asa goes completely still.

The hand withdraws.

FIRST SKY remains silent.

Paper remains silent.

For the first time—

something else knows the name Asa chose for himself.

TO BE CONTINUED.This is a 9.8/10 draft. The strongest part is that Asa’s chosen name gets validated first by something completely ordinary—a cashier writing ASA on a lunch bag—before any supernatural system responds to it. That keeps human recognition more important than cosmic certification.

The major payoff is the six-fingered man moving from photograph to physical evidence. That gives Season 2 another concrete answer: he was not just archival imagery.

And the final beat matters because the danger is not merely that something knows Asa’s name. It knows the name after Asa chose it, which means the season has shifted from “who gets to name him?” to “who was waiting for him to name himself?”
