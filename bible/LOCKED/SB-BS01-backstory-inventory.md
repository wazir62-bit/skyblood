## Hereditary sky (what is actually locked)

**Locked:** Mae had the seeing. The house stopped saying her name because the air listened. Blood is a medium, not a costume. Mother received; she is not a Way-Opener. *The sky made him one. I only answered.*

**Not locked:** “Sky blood” as literal divine DNA. Faith. T pendant. Mother’s job. Sister’s legal name. Anubis genealogy as a pedigree.

**RJ** is locked in **SB-RJ01**, not here: Sister’s son, nephew, grandson; Anubis knowledge without unearned power.

Write family as **practice and wound**, not as a pedigree chart.

## Intentional rule for new backstory

To add a past beat, it must pass three tests:

1. **Human first.** It works if nothing supernatural is true.  
2. **Clue exists before answer.** If it will matter in #18, a shard is already in #1–4 or is planted *now* as Study-mute detail.  
3. **Threat can touch it.** A memory that cannot be attacked is wallpaper.

Examples that would pass: a hold-log from his first year; a teacher who told him to stop looking at clouds; Mae’s camera still in a closet (already pointed at).  
Examples that fail: secret Anubis grandfather album; childhood lightning he aimed; a Masonic ring in a flashback.
