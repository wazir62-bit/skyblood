# SB-CB01 — SKYBLOOD Color & Lighting Bible

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Writer** | Grok (tightened lock from approved production sheet) |
| **Status** | **LOCK** |
| **Applies** | Series-wide unless superseded by an explicit later canon lock |
| **Cross-reference** | SB-PB01 · SB-WD01 Rev. 2 |

---

## 1. Core law

Color does not identify Elias Marrow as a superhero.

SKYBLOOD assigns visual meaning by office:

| Office | Visual language | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Room** | Fluorescent / practical environmental light | Physical truth of the location |
| **Shift** | Fluorescent yellow-green lime | Labor, visibility, issued PPE |
| **Return** | Silver retroreflective material | Existing light returned |
| **Classification** | Beige / gray / black ink | Institutional record and administrative certainty |
| **Recognition** | Recognition-gold | An event occurring at an eligible boundary |

These systems may coexist. They do not merge.

---

## 2. Recognition-gold

Gold is not Elias’s color. Gold is an event.

Recognition-gold is light using an eligible reflective or boundary medium.

Eligible examples: glass; water; wet pavement; approved reflective industrial surfaces; eligible metal; retroreflective material when physically justified.

The underlying object does not transform. Glass remains glass. Water remains water. Silver remains silver.

Gold arrives. Gold leaves.

Recognition-gold is never: Elias’s aura; costume trim; stored energy; a transformation state; emotional glow; a weapon; an attack; a beam; electricity; fire; generic magic.

> Gold may identify an event. It may never costume the man.

---

## 3. Body prohibition

Recognition-gold does not inhabit the human body.

Forbidden unless a later explicit canon lock overturns this rule: gold eyes or pupils; gold skin; gold veins; gold blood; gold tears; gold wounds; gold hair; glowing hands; gold breath; gold aura; supernatural gold body-outline; heroic gold rim-light implying supernatural possession of Elias.

Skin stays skin. Blood stays blood. Eyes stay human. The man does not shine.

---

## 4. Ordinary gold

The physical world may contain ordinary gold-colored objects: jewelry; brass; fixtures; packaging; signs; decorative objects; ordinary warm metallic surfaces.

These are not recognition events.

> Metallic gold ≠ recognition-gold.

Recognition-gold is distinguished by behavior, context, eligible medium, and established visual grammar.

---

## 5. Lime — the shift

Fluorescent yellow-green belongs to labor.

Elias’s Class 2 vest is: issued PPE; mesh; shared with other workers; functional; replaceable; non-mythological.

Lime means: **SEE THIS WORKER SO YOU DO NOT HIT HIM.**  
It does not mean: **THIS MAN HAS BEEN CHOSEN.**

Forbidden: gold-and-lime superhero suit; custom mythological vest; symbols or hieroglyphs; FIRST SKY insignia; armored lime plating; cape; magical H-wrap; permanent off-shift use as Elias branding.

Off shift, the vest comes off unless the scene independently requires PPE.

Lime belongs to the clock, not the man.

---

## 6. Silver — return

The H-wrap remains silver retroreflective material.

Silver does one thing: **it returns existing light.**

Recognition-light may interact with eligible reflective tape under established canon conditions. It does not permanently recolor it. When the event ends, silver remains silver.

Never convert the H-wrap into gold costume trim.

---

## 7. Paper — classification

Paper’s visual world remains deliberately mundane: beige; gray; white; black ink; ordinary toner; manila; dull metal; low-saturation fluorescent interiors.

Paper must never become visually occult merely because its information is extraordinary.

Forbidden: glowing documents; magical parchment; gold ink; gold foil; mystical runes; illuminated hieroglyphs; supernatural filing systems; enchanted-office effects.

> The file should look less important than what it contains.

---

## 8. Fire is not gold

This distinction is absolute.

Fire orange = combustion. Recognition-gold = recognition/address event.

Fire may naturally illuminate Elias. It must never be confused with a recognition event.

Fire burns. Gold notices.

---

## 9. Location lighting

Every location begins with physically motivated light.

**Hospital / waiting room** — cold institutional: fluorescent green-white, gray, vinyl, navy scrubs. Recognition-gold, if present, remains restricted to an eligible boundary.

**Cargo apron / lot** — wet industrial: charcoal, navy, concrete, lime, silver, yellow ground markings, work lights. Yellow paint remains ordinary industrial paint.

**Paper / record spaces** — dead administrative: beige, gray, black, white, low-saturation fluorescent.

**Fire / industrial emergency** — hot practical: orange, soot, emergency red, smoke, dirty white practical light. No recognition-gold merely because the scene is warm.

**Home / family** — human and ordinary: skin, wood, lamps, daylight, household surfaces. Home does not become mystical because Elias enters it.

---

## 10. Multiple temperatures

Different physically motivated color temperatures may coexist (ambulance outside a hospital; sunset through industrial glass; sodium against fluorescent; kitchen against daylight).

> Competing color temperatures may coexist when physically motivated, but symbolic color must never override the truth of the location. Recognition-gold still requires an eligible boundary.

---

## 11. Diegetic first

Before adding symbolic light, ask: where is the light physically coming from?

Possible sources: fluorescent fixtures; headlights; apron lights; screens; daylight; household lamps; emergency vehicles; fire.

Recognition-gold does not replace these sources. It occurs within them.

---

## 12. Elias is not hero-lit by default

Do not beautify Elias simply because he is the protagonist.

Avoid habitual: gold rim-light; supernatural facial bounce; glowing silhouettes; divine backlighting; magical eye catches; spotlight-like recognition.

If Elias stands in bad warehouse light, he may look like a man standing in bad warehouse light. His actions provide the heroism.

---

## 13. Action law

Established superhuman action does not automatically change the palette.

When Elias uses strength, speed, durability, or another locked ability: no automatic gold; no aura; no energy trail; no supernatural color shift; no costume illumination.

Power is shown through consequence: bent steel; displaced weight; torn soles; damaged equipment; acceleration; impact; bodily strain; environmental reaction.

The reader should be able to think **Elias did that** without the page implying **FIRST SKY did that.**

---

## 14. Thumbnail / cover law

At thumbnail scale, one visual office may dominate:

- Worker image: lime H against industrial dark.
- Recognition image: gold on an eligible boundary.
- Paper image: administrative geometry, beige and black.

Do not repeatedly combine dominant lime Elias with dominant recognition-gold until they become a recognizable superhero palette.

SKYBLOOD must never accidentally manufacture a gold-and-lime costume through repetition.

Covers may heighten composition. They may not violate canon.

Forbidden cover shortcuts: gold Elias; gold eyes; gold skin; gold aura; gold fists; gold lightning; lime-and-gold hero suit; FIRST SKY staged as a conventional patron deity; Paper rendered as magical bureaucracy.

---

## 15. Other colors

SKYBLOOD is not literally restricted to these colors. The world remains a world.

Red blood, green trees, blue sky, orange fire, purple signage, gold jewelry, police lights, and ordinary clothing may exist.

The restriction concerns symbolic ownership, not ordinary color.

> Other colors may exist diegetically. They do not gain a symbolic office without a new canon lock.

---

## 16. Production check

Before approving a finished page, verify:

1. Where does every major light physically originate?
2. If recognition-gold appears, what eligible boundary carries it?
3. Has recognition-gold touched skin, eyes, blood, hair, wounds, or aura?
4. Is lime functioning as PPE rather than hero branding?
5. Is silver still silver?
6. Can fire be mistaken for recognition-gold?
7. Has Paper become visually magical?
8. Is Elias receiving hero lighting without physical justification?
9. Does physical consequence communicate his power without supernatural color?
10. At thumbnail scale, does Elias remain a worker/person rather than “the gold superhero”?

Any violation requires revision unless explicitly superseded by later canon.

---

## Master color law

Fluorescent is the truth of the room.  
Lime is the truth of the shift.  
Silver is the truth of returned light.  
Beige and black are the truth claimed by the file.  
Recognition-gold is the truth admitted by a boundary.

No office owns Elias.

---

## Series line

> Gold is the sky noticing. It is never the man shining.
