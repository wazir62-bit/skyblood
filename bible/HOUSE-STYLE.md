# House style (production copy)

When writing bible sheets or issue captions:

- Metadata block at top (Writer / Status / Depends on).
- **En dashes** (–), not spaced hyphens for ranges and asides.
- **Page 24** always capitalized as a named page.
- No raw hex colors in bible copy (say lime, charcoal, navy, silver-gray).
- Vest is **H-wrap**, never “and/or X/chevron.”
- Gold is **recognition-light**, never a crayon.
- “Receiver” never means a wet vest or a pane of glass.
