# Craft — Superhero birth doctrines

**Status:** discussion. SD01 remains the lock. This does not rewrite it.  
**Does not** open Issue #8.

---

## What “birth” means here

SD01, one sentence:

> We are not watching a superhero’s adventures. We are witnessing the events that make a man become one.

Birth is **not** a costume, a title, a lightning strike, or a grandfather.  
Birth is a **sequence of decisions under threat**, each of which leaves him unable to go back to the man who only clocked in.

He can still clock in. That is the point. The job stays. The man does not.

## SD01’s actual birth engine

Not a once. A **cycle**.

Mystery → Threat → Reaction (as the ordinary man he **currently** is) → Discovery → Action → Cost → Climax → He is different (**not necessarily stronger**) → Deeper mystery.

The parenthetical is the doctrine most origin comics drop. They confuse transformation with **upgrade**.

| Book | What was born | What was not |
|---|---|---|
| #1 | He turns back. Photographs. Hears a title. Does not use it. | A guardian. |
| #2 | A mouth that will say a name in a kitchen. | A bloodline costume. |
| #3–4 | A man who will not write a stolen Close. | A closed scale. |
| #5 | Last off the field **as a choice**. | A scout. |
| #6 | A thief of a theft. Human hand. | A team. |
| #7 | He can lose paper and keep a *ren*. | Victory. |

#8 (if marked): a man who still walks toward danger when **the one who walked toward him is gone**. That is not a power. That is a spine.

## Four laws of this birth (already implied; not a new sheet)

**1. Threat makes the stage, not lore.**  
Mythology creates questions. Danger forces answers. A Ptah essay is not an origin beat.

**2. The ordinary body stays the instrument.**  
Vest, latch, judgment, road home. If the climax needs a gust, the birth failed.

**3. Cost lands on someone else as soon as it can.**  
#7: they take paper. #8 (pitch): they take W’s nights without touching him. Collateral is how a public hero is born. Private refusal is still a worker protecting himself.

**4. He is different; the mystery is larger.**  
If only the mystery grows, he is a detective. If only he grows, the iceberg dies. Both, every book.

## False births (do not stage)

- Cape in the locker.  
- Saying Way-Opener as acceptance.  
- Gold eyes as graduation.  
- Anubis as mentor.  
- “I understand everything now.”  
- Getting W back by becoming first-out (that would birth **their** scout).  
- A second receiver so he isn’t lonely.  
- Scale ticks because he was brave.

Bravery that fills their form is not birth. It is recruitment.

## #8 against the doctrine (when you mark write)

The owner’s test is the correct one:

Does he still move toward danger when nobody is moving toward him?

That is closer to “with great power” than a storm would be — and he **still does not have the power**. The ethic arrives before the gift. That is the SKYBLOOD inversion of the Marvel sentence.

Being right does not save him. That is the horror inversion of the chosen-one sentence.

W’s removal looking reasonable is the competing-truth inversion of the origin villain: the world does not need a green goblin to isolate the hero. It needs a safety policy.

If #8 ends with a new ability, we will have used the isolation as an XP dump. If it ends with him smaller and still turning back, something has actually been born.

## One line

A superhero is born the first time he pays **someone else’s** cost and still uses the same body tomorrow.

Not locked as a new sheet. SD01 already said it. #8 is where we find out if we meant it.
