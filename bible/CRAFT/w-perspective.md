# Craft — W’s perspective

**Status:** analysis. Not a lock. Name stays **W**. Does not open #10.

W is not a sidekick. He is not a corpse. He is the first person on the floor who **walked toward** Elias, and the first person the system could move without a punch.

---

## Who he thinks he is

Night lead. Sort. Hours. A board that should match the sky and often doesn’t.

He does not think in temples. He thinks in **pressure, paint, and who is last**.

When Elias says the air is wrong, W does not hear a god. He hears a man who has been early before. *I’ll keep an eye.* That is the whole theology: **watch with him**, don’t file him.

#1: everyone else moved away. W moved in. From W’s side that was not destiny. It was the job. You do not leave a man on the stand if the weather is lying.

---

## What he refuses (his own 30B)

He will not sign their team. He will not be first-out. He will not let a form make him Elias’s pole.

*I clocked in. That’s the part.*  
Description, not address. He will write that Elias was there. He will not write a title.

On days, after they take his nights:

*They’ve got numbers. Not mad at you.*  
*Dates get wrong.*

From W’s chair the restriction is **safety with a printout**. He has seen worse clerks. He will not become Elias’s conspiracy. If he did, he would stop being W and start being a plot.

That is loyalty: he will not let Elias spend him as proof.

---

## What #8 costs him (his book, not Elias’s)

Nights were his road. Days are the other one. Wrong light. Same vest. A sort that is not the horn.

He is not fired. Nobody threatened him. That is why it hurts. You cannot fight a reasonable Ops with a latch.

He still has a body, a clock, a kid somewhere on the far paint he is not standing beside.

He does not get a last-page speech about sacrifice. He gets *Dates get wrong.* That is the most W sentence in the series: **do not make the sky out of a typo.**

---

## How he sees Elias

Not a superhero. A man who is **early**, and who will turn back when a body is still on the paint.

Useful. Dangerous to stand next to. Worth standing next to anyway — until the log said otherwise.

W’s fear (unspoken): if Elias is right too often, **everyone near him becomes a number**. W already is. He is trying not to teach Elias that isolation is cooler.

He would rather Elias be boring and present than right and alone.

---

## What he must not become in later books

- A mentor in a cape’s shadow.  
- A martyr whose injury unlocks a power.  
- The crew-gap. He is living. Do not use him as the Unweighed.  
- The man who “understood the myth.” He understood **hours**.

If he comes back, he comes back as W: days, numbers, not mad, still watching if the board will let him.

---

## One line

W is the other road: a witness who walked toward Elias for the job, paid for the log, and will not let either book turn him into a symbol.
