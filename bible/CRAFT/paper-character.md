# Craft — Paper as a character

**Status:** suggestion / production-facing. **Not a lock.**  
**Does not** give Paper a face, a name, a voice, or a jackal.  
**Does not** open Issue #8.

Paper is not a person. In SKYBLOOD it behaves like one.

SD01 already named the job:

> He keeps a record. They **file** it.

Issue #1 already named the method:

> Erasure looks like paperwork.

---

## Who Paper is

Paper is the **body of the competing book**.

The contractor, the official, the stamp, the FIRST-OUT form, the team form, the manila folder, the photocopy, the envelope at the door — those are **hands**. Paper is what they put on the table and call a person.

It wants:

- a living name attached to a function
- a copy of Mae that it can own
- a crew listed as a team
- a Close without a mouth
- the kitchen treated as a filing cabinet

It cannot:

- make the pans tick
- make the wash keep its shape
- take a *ren* that was already spoken in the house
- become a vest, a title, or a sky

Mother, Issue #7:

> You took paper.  
> You didn’t take her.

That is Paper’s limit, spoken.

## Voice

Paper does not speak. People speak **for** it.

| Speaker | Line | What they think Paper is |
|---|---|---|
| Contractor | “You don’t have to sign. It’s already a record.” | Paper as Close |
| Contractor | “You made a team. We filed a team.” | Paper as crew |
| Official | “Records retrieval. The photocopy is company property.” | Paper as Mae |
| Official | “Retrieved.” | Paper as victory |
| Sister | “If paper is all they want—” | Paper as ransom (wrong) |
| Mother | “Paper is never all they want.” | Paper as bait |
| Elias | “You retrieved paper.” | Paper as paper |

Elias’s line is the character study: he will **describe** Paper. He will not address it as a person. Description, not title.

| Book | Paper’s move | What it fails |

| #2 | The print was already washed | A kitchen says Mae |
| #3 | Clipboard wants the next line | He will not write it |
| #4 | Stolen Close, no name | Ink beads. Paper knows it is a theft |
| #5 | FIRST-OUT form, unsigned | He stays last |
| #6 | Stamp without signature; file Mae; file the crew | He takes the copy by hand. W and Dez will not sign |

Paper **escalates by believing its own metaphor.** Each time it is refused as a book, it tries to become a **body** (photocopy), then a **place** (the kitchen), then it will want a **mouth**.

Sister is already offering that mouth. Do not spend it until a named book.

## What Elias is to Paper

Not a superhero. A **living line they cannot fill**.

He clocks in (true record).  
He does not sign (refuses false Close).  
He takes a copy by hand (#6) — theft of a theft, still human.  
He lets them take a print (#7) — last at the door, not first at a fight.  
He names the limit: *You retrieved paper.*

If he ever **fights Paper as if it were a person**, they win the metaphor. Keep him ordinary. Vest. Kitchen. Clock.
