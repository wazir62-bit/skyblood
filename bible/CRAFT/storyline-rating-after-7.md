## What is actually working

- Locks are doing their job. He is not a weather mage.
- Paper is a character. Stamp is a mouth. Kitchen is a book. That is superhero origin as administration — the promise of #1 p.3.
- Mother is the best supporting arc: *Delete it* → receiver → *you took paper*.
- Two records (theirs vs pans) is the series thesis and it is **clear**.
- Couplet lettering in #6–7 is a voice. It can also hide thin plot if every page is a proverb.

## He has not gotten stronger. Has he changed?

Yes, but sideways:

- #1 last for Dez (human).  
- #2 asked a name.  
- #3–4 would not write.  
- #5 would not be first.  
- #6 stole paper back.  
- #7 **let them take it** and named the limit: *You retrieved paper.*

That last one is growth: he can lose an object without losing the *ren*. If #8 is “he refuses again,” it is not growth. It is the new costume.
