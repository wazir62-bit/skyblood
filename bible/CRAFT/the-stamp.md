# Craft — The Stamp

**Status:** suggestion / production-facing. **Not a lock.**  
**Does not** make the stamp magic, gold, or a god.  
**Does not** open Issue #8.

Paper is the body. **The stamp is the mouth.**

Issue #6, page 1:

> The line is empty.  
> The stamp is not.

That is the whole character in two lines. A signature is a heart agreeing. A stamp is a tongue that does not wait.

---

## What a stamp is in the living world

A stamp is **authority without a person**.

It does not know the form. It does not read the blank. It repeats. Same red. Same word. PROCESSED. RETRIEVED. FIRST-OUT.

A signature can refuse. A stamp cannot. That is why they love it.

Issue #5 left a line for his hand. He did not write. Issue #6 **does not need the hand**. The stamp fills the office that the mouth refused.

6-26:

> They can stamp a living name.  
> They cannot stamp a mouth.

---

## What a stamp is in the buried offices

Not printed on the page. Study only.

**Ptah:** the heart conceives; the tongue creates. A stamp is a tongue with no heart. False creation. A world spoken into being that the *ib* did not invent.

**Thoth:** the true scribe records what the pans do. A stamp records what the clerks want the pans to have done. False Book.

**Spell 30B:** the heart is told *do not testify against me*. Their stamp is the inversion — the file is told *testify anyway*. Speak a Close the heart will not give.

**Seal / *khetem*:** in Egypt a seal authenticates. A cartouche **protects** a royal *ren*. Their stamp is a cartouche around a name the person did not put in it. Protection as capture.

**Heart scarab:** a seal *on* the heart so it stays quiet. Their stamp is a seal *instead of* the heart.

Do not draw any of that. Draw red ink on a blank line.

## Stamp vs the true marks in SKYBLOOD

| Their mark | True mark |
|---|---|
| PROCESSED over empty signature | Clock-in. He chooses the hub. |
| FIRST-OUT already typed | Last off the field |
| Team form, names filled | W: *I clocked in. That’s the part.* |
| RETRIEVED | Mother: *You took paper.* |
| Countersignature hunger (p.12 → #6) | He will not write the next line (#3) |
| Red pad | Gold is recognition-light, **not ink** (WD01) |
| Stamp as Close | Pans catch. Not a tick. |

Clock-in is the **honest stamp**. Same grammar: a machine marks a living presence. Difference: he walks to it. They bring theirs to a blank.

If a later book ever rhymes them, rhyme **visually** (time-clock vs pad) and let the reader feel it. Do not caption “this is the real cartouche.”

## Color and material (production)

- Pad: ordinary office red or purple. Not gold. Gold on a stamp would spend recognition-light as stationery.
- Word: English bureaucracy. PROCESSED / FILED / RETRIEVED. Not hieroglyphs. Not *maat*.
- Placement: **over or beside** the empty signature. The blank must still be readable. The horror is the coexistence.
- Sound: a wet *thump* if you need SFX. Not thunder. Not a scale tick.

The stamp is industrial. Same family as the clipboard and the lanyard. If it looks occult, you have drawn the wrong office.

---

## Who holds it

Not a named “Stamp-man.”

Hands: contractor, clerk, official. Forgettable. The pad can sit on a desk with no one touching it. Authority that does not need a face — that is the point.

Do not give the stamp a will on-panel. Paper is hungry. The stamp is **how hunger speaks**.

---

## What the stamp cannot do

- It cannot make Mother pick up an envelope.
- It cannot make W countersign a pattern.
- It cannot make Dez a team.
- It cannot make the wash keep a photocopy.
- It cannot tick the pans.
- It cannot stamp a **mouth**. Speech stays in the kitchen. 30B stays in Elias.

It can still hurt. It can put his name in a system. It can send two men to a door in daylight. It can empty a table. Those are costs. They are not a Close.

---

## Dramatic job

The stamp is the moment Paper **stops asking**.

#3–5: they wanted a line, a name, a first pole. He could refuse with a hand.

#6: they stop needing the hand.

That is why Book Six is called *They Filed It* and not *He Signed It*. The title is the stamp’s victory. The last page is the mouth’s refusal: he did not sign. Both are true. Two records.
