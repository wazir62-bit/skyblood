# Neshmet barque

**Status: SUGGESTION.** Ikhernofret’s Great Procession. Not a plot lock. Elias does not board.

The **Neshmet** is Osiris’s festival boat at Abydos. Human-built; it **belongs to Nun**, the primordial water. A cabin holds the shrine. Gold, stone, refurbished by kings. Helping restore it counted as a great good deed. In the mysteries it carries the statue **from temple to tomb and back** — death, then return. That return is resurrection. That return is Close.

Ikhernofret’s second movement: the funeral rides this boat. Enemies are driven from it so the body can travel.

SKYBLOOD rhymes, does not restage:

- pp. 20–21 are the station. The barque would be how you **enter**. Entering is banned.
- Eleven photographs are a man trying to board with a lens.
- Night sort is freight on wet concrete, not a golden hull.
- Contractor’s clipboard is a **counterfeit Neshmet** — names as cargo, no Nun.
- Scale-catch / refused Close = the boat will not take the wrong passenger home.
- Rain, wash, silent strike = Nun without a shrine.

Do not: splash Elias in the Neshmet; gold cabin as costume; caption *this is Nun*; make restoring a boat his power-up.

**One line:** The Neshmet is a handmade boat that belongs to deep water, built so a dead king can leave and come home. SKYBLOOD has shown the house in the cloud and not let him step in.
