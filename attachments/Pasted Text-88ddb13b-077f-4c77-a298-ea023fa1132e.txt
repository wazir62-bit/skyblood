# SB-CL01 — Ceril as Elias Marrow  
**Status:** Draft for approval  
**Age:** 35  
**Cast lock:** Ceril  
**Issue:** SKYBLOOD #1  

## Face and body (source = existing Ceril photos)

- Face is Ceril. Do not redesign him. Do not average him toward a generic comic hero.
- Age presentation: 35. Experienced working man. Not a rookie. Not mid-life. Not 50.
- Body: cargo-hub labor — used to walking the apron, lifting, standing watch. Not gym-hero bulk. Not thin-waisted superhero anatomy.
- Skin, hair, facial hair, hairline, ears, nose, mouth, and eye shape: **copy the reference set**. If a panel disagrees with the photos, the panel is wrong.
- Expression default: contained. He looks first and speaks second. Do not give him a constant scowl or a constant soft-sad look.

**Verify against the photos before approval**

- Hair: length, edge-up, texture, any gray or lack of gray  
- Facial hair: clean / stubble / beard — exact to refs, not “comic beard”  
- Forehead, brows, and eye set  
- Any mole, mark, or asymmetry already on Ceril — keep; do not invent extras  

## Issue #1 wardrobe (visual, not a costume)

- Default: facility work clothes + reflective safety vest.
- Page 24: wet apron, runway geometry, vest reads as accidental emblem. That is found, not designed.
- No superhero suit. No chest symbol. No cape. No gold trim on clothing.
- Gold appears only when the sky recognizes him — not as jewelry, paint, or a logo.

## Do not add without a later approved lock

- Jewelry  
- Scars  
- Tattoos  
- New haircut  
- Glasses  
- Ear gauges / chains / watch as signature prop  

If it is not on Ceril’s photos or in an approved wardrobe board, it stays off.

## Character read (for artists — not a monologue)

Elias is 35. He has already spent years suppressing what he perceives. He moves with the economy of someone who has worked long enough to see trouble early. Restraint is not emptiness. Do not draw him passive-blank.

## Off-model tells (reject these)

- Age 30 pretty-lead  
- Age 50 late-awakening patriarch  
- Marvel jaw / DC lantern jaw overwriting Ceril  
- Costume vest with a designed emblem  
- Gold eyes or gold skin as a default power look  
- “Mid-life” body language notes in captions or model sheets
