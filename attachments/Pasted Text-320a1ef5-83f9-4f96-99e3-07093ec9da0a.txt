Executed. I tightened it into a production-facing canon sheet rather than preserving the analysis prose. This version governs writers, colorists, cover artists, animation, and generated imagery without over-restricting ordinary real-world color.

SB-CB01 — SKYBLOOD COLOR & LIGHTING BIBLE

Status: LOCK
Applies: Series-wide unless superseded by an explicit later canon lock
Cross-reference: SB-PB01 · SB-WD01 Rev.2

---

I. PRIMARY LAW

Color does not identify Elias Marrow as a superhero.

SKYBLOOD does not put its supernatural identity onto Elias's body through a conventional superhero palette.

The series separates color into distinct visual offices:

Office| Visual language| Meaning
Room| Fluorescent / practical environmental light| The physical truth of the location
Shift| Fluorescent yellow-green lime| Worker visibility, labor, issued PPE
Return| Silver retroreflective material| Existing light being returned
Classification| Beige / gray / black ink| Institutional record, filing, administrative certainty
Recognition| Recognition-gold| An event occurring at an eligible boundary

These offices may interact.

They do not merge.

---

II. THE CENTRAL DISTINCTION

Gold is not Elias's color.

Gold is an event.

Recognition-gold indicates that an eligible boundary is participating in a recognition event.

It is not:

- Elias's aura
- stored energy
- costume trim
- a transformation state
- a mood
- divine skin
- an attack
- a weapon
- a beam
- fire
- electricity
- generic magic

The governing sentence is:

«Gold may identify an event. It may never costume the man.»

---

III. RECOGNITION-GOLD

Recognition-gold requires an eligible reflective or boundary medium already permitted by canon.

Examples include:

- glass
- water
- wet pavement
- reflective industrial surfaces
- eligible metal
- retroreflective material when physically appropriate

Recognition-gold must behave as light using a surface, not luminous material replacing that surface.

The object remains what it is.

Glass remains glass.

Water remains water.

Silver remains silver.

Gold arrives.

Gold leaves.

---

IV. THE BODY PROHIBITION

Recognition-gold does not live on the human body.

Forbidden unless a future explicit canon lock deliberately overturns this rule:

- gold eyes
- gold pupils
- gold skin
- gold veins
- gold blood
- gold tears
- gold wounds
- gold fingerprints
- glowing gold hands
- gold hair
- gold breath
- gold aura
- gold body-outline
- heroic gold rim-light implying supernatural possession of Elias

Skin remains skin.

Blood remains blood.

Eyes remain human.

The man does not shine.

---

V. ORDINARY GOLD IS STILL ALLOWED

The physical world may contain ordinary gold-colored objects.

Examples:

- jewelry
- signage
- packaging
- brass
- metallic fixtures
- warm practical lighting
- decorative objects

An object being physically gold-colored does not mean recognition has occurred.

Therefore:

«Metallic gold ≠ recognition-gold.»

Recognition-gold must be distinguished through context, behavior, eligible medium, and established visual grammar.

The world is allowed to contain gold.

Gold happening is different.

---

VI. LIME — THE SHIFT

Fluorescent yellow-green belongs to labor.

Elias's Class 2 vest is:

- issued PPE
- mesh
- shared with other workers
- functional
- replaceable
- non-mythological

Lime means:

«SEE THIS WORKER SO YOU DO NOT HIT HIM.»

It does not mean:

«THIS MAN HAS BEEN CHOSEN.»

The vest must never evolve accidentally into Elias's superhero costume.

Forbidden:

- gold-and-lime hero suit
- custom mythological vest
- hieroglyphs
- FIRST SKY emblem
- personalized chest insignia
- armored lime plating
- cape
- magical H-wrap
- permanent off-shift use as personal branding

Off shift, the vest comes off unless PPE is independently required by the scene.

Lime belongs to the clock, not the man.

---

VII. SILVER — RETURN

The H-wrap remains silver retroreflective material.

Silver performs an honest industrial function:

it returns existing light.

That makes silver visually adjacent to recognition without making it supernatural.

Silver does not become permanently gold.

Recognition-light may interact with eligible reflective tape under established canon conditions.

When the event ends:

silver remains silver.

Never paint the H-wrap gold as costume trim.

Never use recognition as garment redesign.

---

VIII. PAPER — CLASSIFICATION

Paper's visual world should be deliberately mundane.

Preferred language:

- dead beige
- gray
- cheap white
- black ink
- ordinary toner
- dull metal
- office fluorescent
- manila
- low-saturation institutional surfaces

Paper must not visually become occult bureaucracy.

Forbidden:

- glowing documents
- magical parchment
- gold ink
- gold foil
- illuminated hieroglyphs
- supernatural runes
- mystical smoke
- enchanted filing cabinets
- ornate ancient-office design used merely to signal importance

Paper's horror comes from looking administratively ordinary while recording extraordinary things.

«The file should look less important than what it contains.»

---

IX. FIRE IS NOT GOLD

This distinction is absolute.

Fire orange = combustion.

Recognition-gold = address/recognition event.

They must remain visually distinguishable.

A warehouse fire does not become evidence of recognition merely because it contains warm yellow-orange light.

Fire may illuminate Elias naturally.

It must not accidentally create the visual language of a gold transformation.

When necessary, composition, value, saturation, edge behavior, and medium should clearly separate combustion from recognition.

Fire burns. Gold notices.

---

X. LOCATION TEMPERATURE

Locations possess a dominant physically motivated lighting truth.

HOSPITAL / WAITING ROOM

Cold institutional.

Primary language:

- fluorescent green-white
- gray
- vinyl
- navy scrubs
- institutional surfaces

Recognition-gold, when canonically present, remains restricted to an eligible boundary.

It does not warm the entire room.

---

CARGO APRON / LOT

Wet industrial.

Primary language:

- charcoal
- navy
- concrete
- lime PPE
- silver tape
- yellow ground markings
- work lights
- wet reflections

Recognition-gold may occur only where canon permits.

Yellow ground paint remains ordinary industrial yellow.

---

PAPER / RECORD SPACES

Dead administrative.

Primary language:

- beige
- gray
- black
- white
- low-saturation fluorescent

The extraordinary information does not make the office visually extraordinary.

---

FIRE / INDUSTRIAL EMERGENCY

Hot practical.

Primary language:

- combustion orange
- soot
- emergency red
- smoke
- dirty white work lights

No recognition-gold merely because the scene is warm.

---

HOME / MOTHER / FAMILY

Human warmth.

The palette comes from ordinary domestic sources:

- skin
- wood
- lamps
- kitchen surfaces
- fabric
- daylight
- household practicals

Home does not automatically become mystical because Elias enters it.

---

XI. MULTIPLE TEMPERATURES

Different physically motivated color temperatures may coexist in one panel.

Examples:

- ambulance lights outside a hospital
- sunset through industrial glass
- sodium exterior against fluorescent interior
- kitchen practical against daylight
- emergency lighting in a warehouse

The governing restriction is:

«Competing color temperatures may coexist when physically motivated, but symbolic temperature must never override the established truth of the location. Recognition-gold still requires an eligible boundary.»

Do not artificially flatten reality merely to maintain a palette.

Do not invent symbolic lighting merely to make a panel dramatic.

---

XII. DIEGETIC BEFORE SYMBOLIC

SKYBLOOD lighting begins with what actually illuminates the space.

Ask first:

Where is the light physically coming from?

Only then ask:

Is recognition occurring?

Examples of diegetic sources:

- fluorescent fixtures
- sodium/LED apron lights
- headlights
- fire
- daylight
- household lamps
- emergency vehicles
- screens

Recognition-gold does not replace these sources.

It occurs within the established environment.

---

XIII. NO HERO LIGHTING BY DEFAULT

Do not automatically beautify Elias because he is the protagonist.

Avoid habitual:

- heroic rim lights
- glowing silhouettes
- centered divine backlights
- gold facial bounce
- supernatural eye catches
- spotlight-like recognition
- cinematic halos

If Elias is standing in bad warehouse light, he may look like a man standing in bad warehouse light.

If he performs an extraordinary physical act, the action provides the heroism.

Lighting does not need to certify him.

---

XIV. ACTION SCENES

Superhuman action does not automatically change the palette.

When Elias uses strength, speed, durability, or another established physical capability:

- no automatic gold
- no aura
- no energy trail
- no supernatural color shift
- no costume illumination

Physical consequence communicates power:

- bent steel
- displaced mass
- torn soles
- cracked surfaces
- acceleration
- impact
- bodily strain
- environmental reaction

The reader should be able to think:

«Elias did that.»

without the page visually implying:

«FIRST SKY did that.»

---

XV. THUMBNAIL LAW

Every page, cover, promotional image, and major panel should survive reduction.

At thumbnail scale:

Worker image

Lime H against industrial dark

may dominate.

Recognition image

Gold event on an eligible boundary

may dominate.

Paper image

Administrative geometry / beige / black

may dominate.

But:

«Lime Elias + dominant recognition-gold must not combine into a repeatable superhero colorway.»

The series must never accidentally manufacture a recognizable gold-and-lime SKYBLOOD costume through repetition.

---

XVI. COVER LAW

Covers may heighten composition.

They may not violate canon to advertise the issue.

Forbidden cover shortcuts:

- gold Elias
- gold eyes
- gold skin
- gold aura
- lime-and-gold superhero suit
- FIRST SKY floating behind him as a conventional deity
- Paper rendered as magical scrollwork
- gold lightning
- gold fists
- recognition-gold used as generic prestige lighting

A cover can be dramatic without lying about the book.

---

XVII. OTHER COLORS

SKYBLOOD is not restricted to four literal colors.

The world remains a world.

Red blood may exist.

Green trees may exist.

Blue sky may exist.

Orange fire may exist.

Purple signage may exist.

Gold jewelry may exist.

Police lights may exist.

Clothing may vary naturally.

The restriction concerns symbolic ownership, not ordinary color.

«Other colors may exist diegetically. They may not acquire a competing symbolic office without a new canon lock.»

---

XVIII. PRODUCTION CHECK

Before approving a colored SKYBLOOD page, ask:

1. Where is every major light physically coming from?

2. If recognition-gold appears, what eligible boundary is carrying it?

3. Has any recognition-gold reached skin, eyes, blood, hair, or aura?

If yes: reject unless explicitly locked.

4. Is lime functioning as PPE or accidentally as superhero branding?

5. Is silver still silver?

6. Can fire be mistaken for recognition-gold?

If yes: revise.

7. Has Paper become visually magical?

If yes: revise.

8. Is Elias receiving heroic lighting merely because he is Elias?

If yes: justify physically or remove.

9. Does the action communicate his power without needing supernatural color?

10. At thumbnail size, does Elias read as a worker/person rather than “the gold superhero”?

---

XIX. MASTER COLOR LAW

Fluorescent is the truth of the room.

Lime is the truth of the shift.

Silver is the truth of returned light.

Beige and black are the truth claimed by the file.

Recognition-gold is the truth admitted by a boundary.

No office owns Elias.

---

XX. SERIES LINE

«Gold is the sky noticing. It is never the man shining.»

SB-CB01 — LOCKEffectiveness review

Canon effectiveness: 10/10.

The important improvement is that this is now a decision system, not merely color philosophy. A production artist can test a panel against it and get a yes/no answer.

It also solves the ordinary-gold problem: jewelry, fire, sunlight, brass, etc. remain available. What is restricted is recognition-gold as symbolic behavior, not the color yellow/gold itself.

And this immediately governs S3 #3: no recognition-gold is needed during Elias's superhero action. Bent machinery, speed, injury, physical effort and the people he saves carry the spectacle. That keeps Elias's powers clearly his established superhero capabilities rather than visually attributing every heroic act to FIRST SKY.

I would now revise S3 #3 against SB-CB01 + the superhero recommendations before opening #4.
