SKYBLOOD #12 — THE FACE AT THE DOOR

LOCK CANDIDATE — REVISED COMPLETE VERSION

OPEN

Three knocks.

KNOCK. KNOCK. KNOCK.

W stands inside his house looking through the peephole.

On the phone:

ELIAS
Who is it?

W doesn't answer.

ELIAS
W.

W keeps staring.

Then:

W
Man... what the hell you doing here?

ELIAS
What?

W steps away from the door.

W
It's you.

Cut to Elias.

He's standing inside his own house.

Phone in hand.

Grandfather's notebook nearby.

ELIAS
Don't open that door.

W
Wasn't planning on it.

W looks again.

The man outside has Elias's face.

Same height.

Same build.

Same ordinary appearance.

Nothing monstrous.

That's what makes it wrong.

He isn't smiling.

He isn't threatening W.

He is simply standing there.

Still.

ELIAS
I'm coming.

W
Hurry.

---

THE OBJECTIVE

Everything in Issue #12 follows one clean objective:

Elias has to reach W.

Something wearing Elias's face is standing outside W's house.

W is alone.

Elias is across town.

No second investigation.

No archive.

No mythology detour.

Go get W.

---

THE WAIT

Elias drives.

No supernatural shortcut.

Traffic exists.

Red lights exist.

The city doesn't know Elias is having the most impossible night of his life.

W stays on the phone.

He checks the peephole again.

False Elias hasn't moved.

W tries humor because that's W.

W
Your twin ugly.

ELIAS
Good.

W
Need to lose some weight too.

ELIAS
Now you lying.

A little release.

Then silence.

W looks again.

His expression changes.

ELIAS
What?

W
He looking at the peephole.

ELIAS
He been looking at the door.

W
Nah.

Beat.

W
He looking at me.

The false Elias slowly raises one hand.

Places its palm against the door.

No knocking.

No attack.

No supernatural spectacle.

Just a hand.

W backs away.

---

GONE

A vehicle passes outside.

Headlights sweep across W's front window.

For an instant the porch disappears in glare.

Darkness returns.

The false Elias is gone.

W checks.

Nothing.

W
He gone.

ELIAS
Good.

W
No.

W continues looking.

W
That ain't good.

We hold there.

No footprints.

No invisible person walking through the house.

No second paranormal mechanism.

Just absence.

---

ELIAS ARRIVES

Headlights.

Elias pulls up.

W sees him through the window.

ELIAS
I'm outside.

W doesn't open the door.

ELIAS
W.

W
Say something.

ELIAS
What?

W
Something only you know.

Smart.

Elias gives him a small private detail from their work history.

Something meaningless to everyone except these two men.

W opens the door.

Elias enters.

They look at each other.

W
You look worse than the other one.

ELIAS
Keep talking and I'm leaving.

W
Take him with you.

They check the house.

Together.

Nothing.

No intruder.

No broken window.

No hidden supernatural evidence.

Just W's house.

They decide W isn't staying there.

W packs a few things.

---

THE HUMAN COST

W reaches his front door.

Stops.

This is his house.

His things.

His chair.

His food.

His bed.

His life.

But now he looks at the door differently.

W
I can't go home tonight.

Elias doesn't answer immediately.

Because W already is home.

That's the point.

Whatever is happening around Elias has taken something from W without physically injuring him.

Safety.

Normalcy.

Home.

Elias:

ELIAS
You coming with me.

---

THE WINDOW

They step outside.

W locks his door.

Elias walks toward the car.

W doesn't.

ELIAS
Come on.

W is staring back at the house.

Elias turns.

PAGE-TURN REVEAL.

Inside W's living room—

standing behind the front window—

is Elias.

The false Elias.

No explanation for how it got inside.

No broken entrance.

No wet footprints.

Nothing.

Just Elias standing inside W's home while the real Elias stands beside W in the street.

W
You see that?

ELIAS
Yeah.

That one word changes SKYBLOOD.

W isn't imagining it.

Elias isn't imagining it.

They both see the impossible.

The false Elias slowly turns its head toward W.

Not toward Elias.

Toward W.

ELIAS
Get in the car.

They leave.

---

THE ROAD

Memphis at night.

Store windows.

Windshields.

Dark storefront glass.

Puddles.

Side mirrors.

Chrome.

The visual motif is everywhere.

We do not have Elias explain it.

We do not have W formulate a theory.

But careful readers may begin noticing something.

Sometimes, buried in reflections, a human form seems present.

Sometimes it isn't.

Some reflections are completely ordinary.

We don't teach readers a simple code.

W looks forward.

Elias drives.

W
Where we going?

ELIAS
Somewhere with people.

W
That your whole plan?

ELIAS
Right now.

W
Terrible plan.

ELIAS
You got another one?

W doesn't.

Then:

W
Them knocks.

Elias looks at him.

W
Your granddaddy write anything else?

Elias remembers the notebook.

He pulls over somewhere public and well lit.

---

GRANDFATHER'S RULE

Elias opens the notebook.

They search the entries around:

HEARD 3.

Then Elias finds something he hadn't understood before.

DO NOT FOLLOW THE FAMILIAR.

Below:

IT WEARS WHAT OPENS THE DOOR.

W reads it.

W
Could've used that earlier.

Elias studies the sentence.

ELIAS
You didn't open the door.

W
No.

Neither knows what that means.

Maybe “open” doesn't mean a physical door.

Maybe it does.

We don't answer it.

Another entry:

DON'T ANSWER YOURSELF.

W
Your granddaddy couldn't just write normal?

Elias turns the page.

One more line.

ONE OF US DID.

Silence.

No explanation.

---

MOVING AGAIN

They leave.

W will stay with a relative for the night.

Simple.

Human.

Safe, hopefully.

The road takes them beneath an old overpass.

Traffic has thinned.

Elias slows.

Someone is standing in the road.

W sees him first.

W
Elias.

ELIAS
I see him.

The car stops.

Ahead:

false Elias.

Not reflected.

Not hidden.

Physically standing beneath the overpass.

Exactly like Elias.

Same clothes.

Same face.

No monster transformation.

No black eyes.

No supernatural costume.

The terror is resemblance.

The figure looks past Elias.

At W.

Then speaks.

FALSE ELIAS
You opened for me.

W goes still.

Elias gets out.

W grabs his arm.

W
Man, don't.

Elias looks at the figure.

Then at W.

ELIAS
Stay here.

---

ELIAS / ELIAS

This confrontation gets visual space.

Minimal dialogue.

False Elias.

Empty roadway.

Real Elias.

W behind him.

Overpass columns create paired vertical structures around them.

Road lines divide the composition.

For readers studying SKYBLOOD, the geometry matters.

For everyone else:

Two identical men are standing in the road.

Elias approaches.

The false Elias looks at him for the first time.

FALSE ELIAS
You aren't first.

Elias stops.

ELIAS
First what?

Nothing.

The double looks back toward W.

Starts walking.

Elias moves between them.

The double shifts left.

Elias shifts left.

Right.

Elias follows.

Protector.

Protected.

Threat.

No mythology lecture necessary.

ELIAS
You want me?

Nothing.

ELIAS
I'm right here.

The false Elias continues looking at W.

And Elias understands:

Whatever this is—

it isn't here for him.

At least not tonight.

---

CONTACT

The double moves toward W.

Elias shoves it.

His hands make contact.

Real resistance.

Cold.

Heavy.

Physical.

The false Elias looks down at Elias's hands against its chest.

Then looks back into Elias's eyes.

It pushes Elias away.

Hard.

Not superhumanly across the street.

Enough to establish:

this thing can touch him too.

W moves forward.

ELIAS
NO.

W stops.

The double approaches again.

Elias gets up.

Steps between them again.

This is the hero panel.

Very little text.

False Elias opposite him.

W behind Elias.

Road.

Columns.

Night.

A gust crosses the road.

Dust moves.

Trash lifts.

The double reacts.

Just slightly.

Elias notices.

He didn't summon it.

He doesn't understand it.

No hand gesture.

No declaration of power.

The wind gives them a moment.

W grabs Elias.

They get into the car.

Drive.

The false Elias doesn't chase.

It watches.

---

AFTERMATH

Public place.

Bright lights.

People nearby.

Elias and W sit in the car.

Neither speaks for several beats.

W finally:

W
You touched it.

ELIAS
Yeah.

W
What it feel like?

Elias thinks.

ELIAS
Like me if I was dead.

W looks at him.

W
That ain't helpful.

ELIAS
I know.

Beat.

W
Why me?

Elias doesn't have an answer.

That's worse than pretending.

ELIAS
I don't know.

W looks through the windshield.

W
I can't stay at my house.

Elias:

ELIAS
You won't.

No speech.

No promise that everything will be okay.

Just responsibility.

---

WHAT ELIAS LEARNS

The false Elias used his face to approach W.

That means Elias's presence is no longer dangerous only because strange events happen around him.

Something can use Elias himself as access to other people.

His face.

Their trust.

Their familiarity.

That realization hurts more than being attacked.

Elias drops W with family.

Before W gets out:

W
Don't come knocking.

Elias almost smiles.

ELIAS
I'll call first.

W gets out.

Then:

W
Elias.

ELIAS
Yeah?

W
That wasn't you.

Not a question.

Elias:

ELIAS
No.

W
Good.

Beat.

W
Cause I was about to beat your ass.

Door closes.

A little humanity before the final turn.

---

HOME

Elias returns home.

Mother is awake.

She knows something happened immediately.

MOTHER
W okay?

ELIAS
For now.

She sees Elias's face.

MOTHER
What happened?

Elias hesitates.

Then tells her the simplest possible truth.

ELIAS
Something used my face.

Mother doesn't laugh.

Doesn't question whether he imagined it.

Her response is immediate:

MOTHER
Did it speak?

Elias freezes.

ELIAS
How you know to ask that?

Mother looks toward Grandfather's notebook.

Then at her son.

MOTHER
Because your grandfather used to say the worst thing wasn't seeing yourself.

Beat.

MOTHER
It was hearing what it knew.

Elias:

ELIAS
What did it know?

Mother doesn't answer.

END.

ISSUE #12 STORY ENGINE

WANT

Elias wants to reach W and get him somewhere safe.

OBSTACLE

Something wearing Elias's face is already at W's home and appears able to follow their movement.

STAKES

W's safety—and his ability to trust Elias himself.

THREAT

The mystery has acquired Elias's face.

REACTION

Elias goes immediately to W.

DISCOVERY

Both men see the same impossible figure.

ACTION

They leave the house and try to get W somewhere safe.

CLIMAX

The false Elias physically approaches W.

Elias puts himself between them.

CONSEQUENCE

W loses the safety of his own home.

Elias realizes his identity can be weaponized against people who trust him.

TRANSFORMATION

Elias accepts another level of responsibility:

Protecting people from what follows him may become part of protecting them at all.

ONE FOREGROUND SUPERNATURAL QUESTION

What is wearing Elias's face?

Nothing else competes with it.

---

HIDDEN SKYBLOOD ARCHITECTURE

REFLECTION

Reflections appear repeatedly:

windows.

windshields.

mirrors.

chrome.

water.

dark glass.

But characters never announce the pattern.

Some contain possible evidence.

Some contain nothing.

The reader is invited to look without being handed a decoding manual.

THRESHOLDS

W's front door.

Car doors.

Store entrances.

The overpass.

House windows.

The language of crossing continues beneath the plot.

DOUBLE

The false Elias carries several possible symbolic readings:

self/shadow.

known/unknown.

outer/inner.

living/dead.

original/copy.

We lock none of those as the final explanation yet.

MASONIC / ARCHITECTURAL IMAGERY

At the overpass, paired columns frame Elias and the double.

Elias repeatedly occupies the space between paired structures.

No explicit Masonic emblem is required.

Architecture carries the echo.

EGYPTIAN ECHO

The concept of a person possessing more than one aspect can exist underneath the double imagery without naming an Egyptian concept or claiming Egyptian mythology explains the entity.

GREEK ECHO

Recognition, doubles, deceptive forms and identity echo older mythic traditions without becoming exposition.

ILLUMINATED COMPOSITION

The confrontation panel should resemble a modern illuminated image:

architecture framing the scene;

Elias central;

W protected behind him;

the double opposite;

wind/dust forming natural visual ornament.

Nobody comments on it.

---

THE EASTER EGG

When false Elias is revealed inside W's living room, one ordinary object is oriented differently from its position earlier in the issue.

We do not highlight it.

Readers may assume continuity error.

It isn't.

Much later, that object's orientation can help establish exactly when something about the space changed.

---

THE GEM

Grandfather wrote:

ONE OF US DID.

We deliberately protect the ambiguity of US.

Family?

Workers?

People like Elias?

Members of something?

Previous survivors?

Something else?

Later canon decides when the story has earned the answer.

---

ART / PACING LOCKS

The inside-the-house reveal gets a page turn.

The overpass confrontation gets room.

The panel where Elias stands between false Elias and W should be one of the largest panels in the issue.

Dialogue drops substantially during the confrontation.

The false Elias must look like Elias, not an evil redesign.

No black eyes.

No monster face.

No glowing skin.

No visual shorthand telling readers what it is.

Its wrongness comes primarily from behavior, stillness and context.

The final Mother sequence slows back down.

Fewer panels.

Closer framing.

Let:

“Did it speak?”

land before Elias answers.

---

WHAT ISSUE #12 DOES NOT ANSWER

We don't identify the double.

We don't call it a demon, spirit, clone or doppelgänger in canon.

We don't explain “opening.”

We don't explain why W is targeted.

We don't establish that reflections are portals.

We don't explain ONE OF US DID.

We don't explain the gust.

We don't give Elias deliberate weather control.

We don't explain Grandfather's source of knowledge.

We don't identify a secret society.

We don't identify Egyptian, Greek or Masonic traditions as the answer.

The reader understands exactly what happened.

They simply don't understand what it ultimately means.
