Analysis & review

This issue corrects #12's recognition problem immediately: Elias does not identify the photograph's man as the caller. In fact, the caller himself points out the flaw:

> “You don't know what I look like.”



That's important because Season 3 is fundamentally about the danger of turning evidence into conclusions.

The biggest payoff is Abel. We have waited long enough. Season 2 ended with Abel OPEN, a six-fingered hand and two truths unresolved. Season 3 has now expanded that problem through the city, but we cannot keep discussing Abel indefinitely without putting him back on the page.

And Abel returns as a person, not a monster entrance.

The superhero rule holds

Elias finally reaches the address associated with Abel—and immediately hears children in danger.

He chooses the family first.

That's not incidental. It proves that even when Elias is inches away from one of the largest answers in his life, answers still rank below people.

That is becoming his defining heroic ethic.

RJ

RJ gets only Ground Sense progression.

He detects a luggage cart through connected structure.

That's mundane enough to teach the reader the rule:

> RJ can feel load/movement through connected ground or structure.



No geokinesis yet.

No gold.

No Anubis manifestation.

No combat.

Good.

The final footprints

This is the scale rendered physically:

five toes / six toes.

Both proceed down the same corridor.

We don't tell the reader one is real and the other isn't.

And then Abel appears with six fingers.

The contradiction remains open.

Rating

9.9/10.

This is one of the strongest late-season issues because the mythology finally returns to the foreground without taking the superhero out of SKYBLOOD.

The next issue should not dodge the conversation. Elias and Abel finally need to talk. But something important should remain unresolved: Abel can tell Elias what Abel believes happened. He should not suddenly become an omniscient mythology narrator.

That distinction will determine whether #14 deepens SKYBLOOD or explains it to death.
