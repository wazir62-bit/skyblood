SKYBLOOD

SEASON TWO — ISSUE #12

THAT IS NOT MY GRANDFATHER

Paper kept one record.

Two bodies survived.

Now something wearing Thomas Marrow’s face is standing behind the wall.

---

ACT ONE — THOMAS

1.

2417. 

Night.

The figure stands at the edge of darkness.

Thomas Marrow’s face.

Young.

Exactly as he appears in the old work photograph.

Abel beside Elias.

ABEL: That’s not Thomas.

FIGURE: He said the same thing about you.

---

2.

ELIAS: Step out.

FIGURE: No.

ELIAS: Why?

FIGURE: You won’t like what changes.

Abel looks at Elias.

ABEL: Don’t ask twice.

---

3.

ELIAS: Your name Thomas?

FIGURE: Yes.

ABEL: No.

The figure looks at Abel.

FIGURE: Still doing that.

ABEL: Doing what?

FIGURE: Surviving definitions.

---

4.

Elias studies the face.

ELIAS: What did my grandfather call my mother?

The figure answers immediately.

FIGURE: Baby girl.

Elias gives nothing away.

---

5.

ELIAS: What did he call Mae when he was mad?

The figure pauses.

FIGURE: Mae.

Abel looks at Elias.

Wrong.

Not enough yet.

---

6.

ELIAS: Granddad hated one thing in coffee.

FIGURE: Sugar.

ELIAS: No.

A beat.

ELIAS: He drank it sweet.

The figure’s face does not change.

It simply records the failure.

---

ACT TWO — A RECORD CAN KNOW FACTS

7.

Mother’s kitchen.

Later.

Elias tells the family.

Mother is not impressed by the face.

MOTHER: Ask him something nobody wrote down.

---

8.

SISTER: Like what?

MOTHER: Something stupid.

Everyone looks at her.

MOTHER: Important people leave records.

A beat.

MOTHER: People leave nonsense.

---

9.

Mother thinks.

MOTHER: Ask him about the green chair.

Elias waits.

MOTHER: Don’t tell him anything else.

---

10.

Back at 2417.

Elias:

ELIAS: Green chair.

The figure says nothing.

ELIAS: That mean anything?

FIGURE: Furniture.

Abel exhales.

---

11.

ELIAS: Granddad broke it when Ma was twelve.

FIGURE: No record.

That phrase lands hard.

---

12.

ELIAS: I didn’t ask about a record.

FIGURE: I know.

ELIAS: Then why you answer like one?

The figure finally looks directly at him.

FIGURE: Because that is what survived.

---

ACT THREE — WHAT SURVIVED

13.

Silence.

Abel steps closer to the threshold.

ABEL: Thomas?

FIGURE: Yes.

ABEL: Do you remember me?

FIGURE: Yes.

ABEL: Before closure?

FIGURE: Yes.

ABEL: What did we do the week before?

The figure does not answer.

---

14.

ABEL: We worked together.

FIGURE: Yes.

ABEL: Where’d we eat?

No answer.

ABEL: Who cheated at cards?

No answer.

ABEL: What did Mae call us when we came in late?

The figure’s face tightens.

Not emotion.

Failure.

---

15.

ABEL: You don’t remember.

FIGURE: I contain his retained record.

ELIAS: That ain’t what he asked.

FIGURE: It is the answer.

---

16.

Abel looks devastated.

Not because the figure is fake.

Because it contains enough Thomas to hurt.

---

17.

ABEL: Did he die?

FIGURE: Eventually.

ELIAS: When?

FIGURE: After closure.

ELIAS: Normal death?

FIGURE: Define normal.

W would hate that answer.

---

18.

ELIAS: Did you come from him?

FIGURE: I remained from him.

Different wording.

Important.

---

ACT FOUR — THE RETAINED MAN

19.

Paper activates at Mother’s house.

Dry black ink.

THOMAS MARROW — RETAINED

Then:

BIOLOGICAL STATUS: DECEASED

Mother sees it.

That part is now hard ground.

---

20.

Another line:

RECORD STATUS: ACTIVE

Sister:

SISTER: How can both be true?

Mother:

MOTHER: We been learning that all year.

---

21.

At 2417, Elias receives the text.

He shows Abel.

Thomas died.

The figure still stands there.

---

22.

ELIAS: You’re not his body.

FIGURE: Correct.

ABEL: You’re not his memory.

FIGURE: Incomplete.

ELIAS: You’re not him.

The figure waits.

FIGURE: Define him.

---

23.

Elias:

ELIAS: No.

The figure studies him.

ELIAS: I’m not letting you make the question smaller.

That is Season 2 Elias.

---

24.

FIGURE: Good.

That response bothers Elias more than hostility would.

---

ACT FIVE — THE THING PAPER KEPT

25.

The figure steps one inch closer to the light.

Not out.

Still inside the threshold.

Its face flickers.

For one heartbeat—

not Thomas.

Blank.

Then Thomas again.

Abel sees it.

---

26.

ABEL: Do that again.

FIGURE: No.

ABEL: That face ain’t yours.

FIGURE: It is the face attached to the surviving record.

---

27.

ELIAS: So Paper made you.

FIGURE: No.

ABEL: FIRST SKY?

FIGURE: No.

ELIAS: Then what?

The figure:

FIGURE: The record remained.

A beat.

FIGURE: Something eventually answered it.

---

28.

That is enough.

No taxonomy.

No cosmic species.

Just a mechanism-shaped mystery.

---

29.

ELIAS: Why stay at 2417?

FIGURE: Because this is where Thomas’s surviving record still points.

ELIAS: Address.

The figure says nothing.

Close enough.

---

30.

ABEL: Why call me back?

FIGURE: Because Thomas’s record cannot close cleanly while you remain open.

Abel stiffens.

ELIAS: Paper says Abel is eligible to reopen.

FIGURE: Yes.

ELIAS: You want that?

FIGURE: No.

---

31.

ABEL: Why?

The figure looks at him.

FIGURE: Because if Abel reopens—

A beat.

FIGURE: Thomas stops being the only answer.

Abel understands first.

ABEL: And you stop being Thomas.

The figure says nothing.

That is answer enough.

---

32.

Mother’s kitchen.

Paper activates.

ABEL — CLOSED

Then:

REOPENING ELIGIBLE

Then:

THOMAS MARROW — RETAINED

Then a new procedural line:

RECORD CONFLICT ANTICIPATED

At 2417—

the Thomas-faced figure finally steps backward into the dark.

Not threatening.

Afraid.

Elias watches it go.

ELIAS: That thing scared of you.

ABEL: No.

ELIAS: You sure?

Abel looks toward the darkness.

ABEL: It’s scared of two answers.

Cut to Paper:

RESOLUTION PENDING

No gold.

No FIRST SKY.

Just Paper.

Waiting.
