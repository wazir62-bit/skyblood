The next issue should pay something concrete, as promised. I’m making Season 2 #5 the first hard-evidence issue: Elias and the double test whether they are physically the same person. The answer is no—but not in the way anybody expects.

SKYBLOOD

SEASON TWO — ISSUE #5

SAME BLOOD, WRONG MAN

Two men have Elias Marrow’s face.

The blood says they are related.

It does not say they are the same.

---

ACT ONE — HARD EVIDENCE

1.

Morning.

Mother’s kitchen.

Grandfather’s notebook remains open to:

WHEN TWO TRUTHS ARRIVE TOGETHER, DO NOT MAKE THEM CHOOSE.

Beneath it:

THE SKY WILL.

Nobody is reading it now.

For once—

they are looking at something simpler.

Two sealed specimen tubes.

---

2.

SISTER: This your big supernatural plan?

ELIAS: Blood test.

SISTER: That’s disappointingly normal.

W: Normal is having a good week.

The double stands near the doorway.

Not inside the family circle.

Not outside it either.

---

3.

ELIAS: We keep asking what he is.

DOUBLE: You keep asking.

ELIAS: Fine.

He points to the tubes.

ELIAS: Today we ask something smaller.

DOUBLE: What?

ELIAS: Whether you’re me.

---

4.

The double looks at the blood kit.

DOUBLE: You already know I’m not.

ELIAS: You know.

DOUBLE: Yes.

ELIAS: I don’t.

A beat.

ELIAS: Not yet.

---

5.

Mother:

MOTHER: And if it says you’re the same?

ELIAS: Then we know something.

MOTHER: And if it says you’re not?

Elias looks at the double.

ELIAS: Then we know something.

DOUBLE: That simple?

ELIAS: No.

A beat.

ELIAS: That useful.

---

ACT TWO — THE DRAW

6.

A small independent clinic.

Nothing mystical.

Fluorescent lights.

Plastic chairs.

Bad daytime television.

A receptionist who does not care about destiny.

---

7.

The double sits beside Elias.

Same face.

The receptionist looks from one to the other.

RECEPTIONIST: Twins?

ELIAS: Something like that.

DOUBLE: No.

They answer at the same time.

The receptionist stops caring.

---

8.

Blood draw.

Elias first.

Needle.

Tube.

Label:

ELIAS MARROW

Ordinary red blood.

Nothing happens.

---

9.

The double next.

Needle enters.

For half a second—

nothing.

Then every reflective surface in the room catches gold.

Cabinet glass.

Phone screen.

Metal tray.

Window.

Never skin.

Never eyes.

---

10.

The phlebotomist notices the light.

PHLEBOTOMIST: Power flicker?

ELIAS: Probably.

The double looks at Elias.

They both know better.

---

11.

The tube fills.

Same red.

The label printer activates.

The technician types.

DOUBLE: Don’t use his name.

PHLEBOTOMIST: Excuse me?

DOUBLE: On mine.

---

12.

ELIAS: He’s right.

PHLEBOTOMIST: Then what name?

Silence.

The double has no answer.

That hurts more than he expected.

---

13.

The technician shrugs.

Writes:

JOHN DOE

The instant the marker leaves the label—

the gold reflections stop.

Elias notices.

So does the double.

---

ACT THREE — THE RESULT

14.

Later.

A small lab office.

The technician looks confused.

Not frightened.

Confused.

TECH: So.

ELIAS: That’s never a good opening.

---

15.

TECH: These samples are related.

W: Related how?

TECH: Closely.

ELIAS: Twins?

TECH: No.

DOUBLE: Same person?

TECH: No.

Silence.

---

16.

TECH: First sample is male.

ELIAS: Good start.

TECH: Second sample is also male.

SISTER: We gathered that.

TECH: Same maternal line.

Mother looks up.

That lands differently.

---

17.

TECH: Extremely close overlap.

ELIAS: How close?

TECH: Closer than brothers.

W: But not twins.

TECH: Not twins.

ELIAS: Then what?

The technician turns the screen.

---

18.

TECH: Genetically—

he points to Elias—

you are upstream of him.

No one speaks.

ELIAS: Say that in English.

---

19.

TECH: His sample reads like it descends from yours.

DOUBLE goes still.

SISTER: Descends how?

TECH: I don’t know.

He looks at the ages.

TECH: That’s the problem.

---

20.

ELIAS: Son?

TECH: No.

ELIAS: Clone?

TECH: No.

DOUBLE: What am I?

The technician looks at him.

TECH: Not something this test can name.

---

ACT FOUR — THE PART THAT DOESN’T MATCH

21.

Elias leans closer to the report.

ELIAS: What’s different?

TECH: One region.

ELIAS: Which one?

TECH: Doesn’t make sense.

W: We’ve had a lot of that lately.

---

22.

TECH: There’s sequence information in his sample that isn’t present in yours.

ELIAS: Mutation?

TECH: Maybe.

DOUBLE: From where?

TECH: That’s not how I’d phrase it.

---

23.

The technician highlights the mismatch.

TECH: It’s organized.

ELIAS: Meaning?

TECH: Mutations don’t usually look intentional.

Nobody likes that word.

---

24.

The double stands.

DOUBLE: We’re done.

ELIAS: Sit down.

DOUBLE: We’re done.

ELIAS: You wanted proof.

DOUBLE: No.

He looks at the screen.

DOUBLE: You did.

---

25.

ELIAS: You knew this?

DOUBLE: Not like this.

ELIAS: What did you know?

DOUBLE: That I came after you.

ELIAS: After how?

The double looks toward the window.

Gold has not returned.

DOUBLE: I don’t know.

---

ACT FIVE — SOMETHING IN THE BLOOD

26.

Back at Mother’s house.

The report is on the kitchen table.

Grandfather’s notebook beside it.

Two kinds of evidence.

Neither explains the other.

---

27.

Mother reads the maternal-line result.

MOTHER: Same mother?

ELIAS: Same line.

MOTHER: That ain’t what I asked.

Elias looks at the double.

DOUBLE: I remember her.

Mother goes still.

---

28.

MOTHER: Me?

DOUBLE: Yes.

MOTHER: From when?

DOUBLE: A lot of when.

She studies his face.

MOTHER: You remember being born?

The double’s expression changes.

DOUBLE: No.

---

29.

MOTHER: You remember being little?

DOUBLE: Pieces.

MOTHER: First day of school?

DOUBLE: Pieces.

MOTHER: Your father?

The double says nothing.

MOTHER: Elias remembers him.

DOUBLE: I know.

MOTHER: Then say his name.

The double cannot.

Not pain.

Not bleeding.

Nothing.

Just absence.

---

30.

Elias:

ELIAS: What do you remember first?

The double looks at him.

A long time.

DOUBLE: Waking up.

ELIAS: Where?

DOUBLE: Standing.

ELIAS: Where?

The double finally answers.

DOUBLE: Behind you.

---

31.

Silence.

W: When?

DOUBLE: Before you saw me.

ELIAS: How long before?

DOUBLE: I don’t know.

ELIAS: What did you see?

The double looks toward the refrigerator photograph.

Mae.

Then Grandfather’s notebook.

Then Mother.

DOUBLE: Everything you were about to lose.

Mother’s face changes.

---

32.

Night.

The lab.

Closed.

Empty.

The two blood tubes remain in refrigerated storage.

One labeled:

ELIAS MARROW

One:

JOHN DOE

The refrigerator light flickers.

Paper slides from a printer nobody turned on.

Dry black institutional ink.

MARROW, ELIAS — SOURCE

Below it:

UNASSIGNED DERIVATIVE — ACTIVE

A second line begins typing.

Stops.

Starts again.

Stops.

Paper cannot close the record.

On the reflective steel refrigerator door—

gold appears.

FIRST SKY watches the same two tubes.

Paper has classified the relationship.

FIRST SKY still has not chosen.

And inside the tube marked JOHN DOE—

one red blood cell moves against the flow.

Then another.

Not upward.

Toward Elias’s sample.

TO BE CONTINUED.Review: 9.7/10

This issue does what Season 2 needed: it gives the reader hard ground.

The double is not genetically identical to Elias.

He is also not just a random copy.

The most important concrete result is:

> “You are upstream of him.”



That gives us a relationship without solving origin.

The lab can tell us the double derives from Elias somehow, but not whether that derivation is biological, temporal, engineered, metaphysical, or something FIRST SKY/Paper caused.

That is a real payoff.

The other major improvement is the double’s label problem:

> JOHN DOE



The gold stops only after Elias’s name is not applied to him.

That suggests naming is not just social bookkeeping. The system reacts differently when the double is not falsely assigned Elias’s human name.

But I would not yet declare that a universal rule.

The strongest character material is Mother questioning memory. She does something the lab cannot:

> You remember being born?
You remember being little?
Your father?



That begins distinguishing inherited memory from lived memory.

And the answer:

> “Behind you.”



is stronger than another “future” clue.

It suggests the double’s first stable self-awareness may have begun already attached to Elias’s path.

One thing I would still flag

The phrase:

> “sequence information… organized”



is intentionally provocative, but it needs careful handling later. We should not drift into technobabble genetics where a normal lab can somehow decode alien design. The technician should only be able to say:

this difference is unusual and structured enough to bother me.

Not explain why.

Best mythology beat

Paper:

> MARROW, ELIAS — SOURCE
UNASSIGNED DERIVATIVE — ACTIVE



That is dangerous because Paper has done something FIRST SKY has not.

It has classified the relationship.

But it still cannot close it.

That keeps Paper competent without making it omniscient.
