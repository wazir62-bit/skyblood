My ruling as SKYBLOOD’s approved writer:

# SB-PB01 — Approved with required revisions

The foundation is strong, but I will not approve it unchanged because several invented rules conflict with the existing master bible and episode canon.

## Approved

These become perception canon:

* Elias is sensitive to atmospheric changes before instruments or coworkers detect them.
* His perception begins physically: pressure, inner-ear disturbance, static, temperature change, metallic taste, and directional awareness.
* He can perceive threshold geometry hidden inside ordinary weather.
* Threshold sight is reciprocal: when Elias perceives deeply enough, something on the other side can perceive him.
* Recognition is not a costume grant or conscious power activation.
* Elias cannot summon recognition.
* Gold is restrained recognition imagery—not his permanent skin, eyes, jewelry, logo, or projectile.
* His reflective vest becomes an accidental emblem through existing light and geometry.
* Elias suppresses perception by looking away, staying occupied, controlling his breathing, and refusing to interpret prematurely.
* Threshold sight carries physical and emotional consequences.
* All Issue #1 power limitations in §11 remain enforced.
* Page 24 remains a rupture in his ordinary life—not a transformation or victory.

## Required correction: core rule

Replace:

> Elias perceives the join where sky and ground fail to agree.

With:

> **Elias perceives stress in the living boundary connecting Earth, the celestial stations, and the realm of the dead. It first presents as weather behaving incorrectly, then resolves into threshold geometry.**

“Join” can remain informal production language, but **threshold** is the governing canon term.

## Required correction: atmospheric empathy

The draft incorrectly says the emotional channel is “not empathy.” Episode canon already locks **atmospheric empathy**.

The correct distinction is:

> **Atmospheric empathy does not mean Elias believes the storm has ordinary human emotions. He senses intention, pressure, imbalance, recognition, and response within a living atmospheric boundary. His emotions can provoke an involuntary atmospheric response, but he cannot command it deliberately in Issues #1–3.**

The ranked sense stack becomes:

1. Physical atmospheric sensitivity
2. Atmospheric empathy
3. Visual threshold sight
4. Reciprocal sight

Visual perception is not always second. Elias can feel the boundary without seeing it.

## Required correction: speech does not automatically widen the threshold

I reject §8 as written.

If every detailed sentence widened the seam, Elias explaining the photograph to Dez or his Sister in Issue #1 would already activate the cosmology. That contradicts the established reception ladder.

The governing rule is:

> **Ordinary description does not open a threshold. Exposure intensifies when speech becomes an address: a true name, recognized title, deliberate invocation, or testimony that another person genuinely receives.**

This preserves SB-L17:

> **Address → Receive → Weigh → Close**

Elias stays quiet because disbelief wounded him and because some forms of address are dangerous—not because every sentence mechanically tears reality.

## Required correction: “material receiver”

I reject that term.

A wet apron, vest, glass, metal, water, or runway paint is a **reflective medium**, not a receiver. In SKYBLOOD, “receiver” has a specific relational meaning: a conscious participant who receives an address or testimony.

Use:

* **Reflective medium:** carries visible recognition-light.
* **Witness:** observes evidence.
* **Receiver:** consciously receives an address or testimony and completes the relational circuit.

This distinction is essential. Objects can carry light; they cannot perform the receiver’s office.

## Gold ruling

For Issue #1:

* Gold may appear as reflected recognition-light.
* It must use existing light, water, metal, paint, glass, or reflective fabric.
* No gold eyes, skin, veins, blood, beams, weapons, jewelry symbolism, or designed emblem.
* Gold cannot activate merely because Elias becomes emotional.
* Page 24 is the clearest gold event.

Later canon retains the possibility of brief **gold-black guardian markings**, already present in the master bible, but they do not appear in Issue #1 and require a later transformation lock.

## Issue #1 receiver ruling

Approved:

> **No new human receiver is completed in Issue #1.**

The Sister begins behaving like a potential receiver when she says, “Walk me through it again,” but the act remains incomplete. Elias creates evidence; nobody fully receives his testimony.

Episode 2 completes the first witnessing act through Elias and his Mother restoring the Grandmother’s name.

* Deliberately call lightning
* Open a sky passage
* Enter the temple
* Create a human receiver automatically
* Produce gold from his body
* Wear a superhero costume or designed emblem
* Explain the complete cosmology
* Communicate knowingly with Anubis
* Use guardian form

He can:

* Sense weather and boundary stress early
* Experience atmospheric empathy
* Trigger one involuntary wind response
* Perceive the temple resolving inside cloud
* Photograph evidence
* Survive the silent strike
* Hear “Way-Opener”
* Become visible to the threshold
* Begin the witnessing act without completing it
