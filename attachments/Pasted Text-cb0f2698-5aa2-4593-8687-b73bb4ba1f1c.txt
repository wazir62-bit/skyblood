Issue #2 should not answer what the double is yet. Issue #1 established that the double fears FIRST SKY. The next escalation is more dangerous: Elias discovers that the double isn't merely copying his appearance—it has begun occupying places in his life before Elias gets there.

I’d title this one SOMEBODY ALREADY CAME.

TO BE CONTINUED.Review: 9.8/10

This advances the season without spending the double's identity.

The central revelation isn't what he is. It's:

> “Because it doesn't think I'm you.”



That's more valuable right now. It establishes that the universe itself distinguishes Elias and the double. So this is no longer merely an impostor story.

The other major progression is W. W isn't mentioned as residue anymore; W returns to the narrative and has changed because of Season 1:

> “I learned something too.”



That makes the relationship feel continuous rather than using W as a continuity checkbox.

The notebook also behaves correctly: Elias consults it and gets nothing new. Grandfather left evidence, not an infinitely convenient supernatural instruction manual.

Strongest sequence: Pages 29–32

FIRST SKY writes RETURN, then RETURNED, but we don't know which Elias it means.

Then the missing third knock is followed by Mother's voice.

The double physically stops Elias.

And for the first time we establish that the double can touch him.

That deserves the silence around it.

Then Page 32 turns the entire issue inside out: there appears to be another Elias at Mother's house—and that one has the vest.

Now visual shorthand is compromised too. Until this point, “no vest” helped distinguish the double. The reader loses that safety at exactly the same time Elias does.

And I would absolutely not reveal whether Page 32 is the same double.

That question should carry directly into Season 2 Issue #3.

Overall: 9.8/10 — draft, not lock.
