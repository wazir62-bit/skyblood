TO BE CONTINUED.Revised rating: 9.8/10

This is significantly better because the characters no longer know more than their evidence permits.

The biggest correction is the six-fingered man's testimony. Previously he effectively explained Paper's closure mechanics. Now he tells us only what he personally remembers:

> “I remember five fingers. I remember waking with six.”



and:

> “Nobody acted like someone was missing. They acted like the question had been answered.”



That's much stronger. Readers can theorize that Paper altered him, that the sixth finger protected him, that another system intervened, or that his own interpretation of the event is wrong.

Sister is now excellent — 10/10

Her reasoning progresses in three stages.

First:

> “Stop looking for the person. Look for what expected them.”



Then:

> “Find something built for two.”



And finally she doesn't merely count bodies. She analyzes spacing.

That's an actual investigative methodology emerging from SKYBLOOD's rules: when records and memories can be altered, examine the physical world for accommodations made for something reality claims wasn't there.

That's valuable enough to become part of Sister's long-term role.

The payroll scene is more disciplined

I especially prefer:

> ASA: That's where we'd put the missing man.
ELIAS: Maybe.
ASA: You don't think so?
ELIAS: I think that's where Paper wants us looking.



Excellent Elias.

He's now sophisticated enough to understand that even evidence of manipulation can itself direct an investigation.

That is Season 2 Elias.

Abel is fixed

We now have two pieces of evidence:

The voice says Abel.

Asa independently writes ABEL.

That's significant.

But the six-fingered man explicitly refuses to accept the conclusion:

> “If that's my name—tell me who gave it to me.”



Perfect.

The sensory response also proves nothing. Summer heat, bicycle chain, pavement, laughter, a porch door, grief—those could be recovered memory, implanted memory, associative contamination, or something traveling through the same mechanism that caused Asa to write the word.

So Abel remains provisional.

I would continue calling him the six-fingered man in internal canon documentation until independent physical evidence connects that name to him.
