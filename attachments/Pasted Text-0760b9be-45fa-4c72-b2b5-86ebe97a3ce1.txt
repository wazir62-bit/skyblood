Because RJ already has the Anubis connection, I would build his powers from Anubis's conceptual territory—passage, judgment, protection of the dead, boundaries, and recognition of what truly belongs where—but translate that into SKYBLOOD rather than giving him generic Egyptian magic.

Most importantly, RJ should not be Elias Jr.

Elias is physical intervention: strength, speed, durability, action.

RJ should be perception + passage.

RJ's core power: TRUE WEIGHT

RJ can perceive when a person, object, name, or record doesn't carry the weight it claims to carry.

Not mind-reading. Not lie detection.

That's important.

Someone can tell RJ a lie and fool him.

But when something is standing in for something else, RJ feels the imbalance.

That's why his first instinct about false Elias works perfectly:

> “That ain't him.”



At first everybody—including the reader—thinks RJ is simply observant.

He is observant.

Later we discover there's another layer.

A copied badge says Elias.

RJ sees: not enough weight.

A record says Malik left.

Malik's body says he didn't.

RJ can eventually perceive that those two things don't balance.

That ties him directly to the scale without putting a magical scale in his hands.

Second ability: THRESHOLD SENSE

RJ knows when a boundary has been crossed incorrectly.

Door. Gate. Grave. Property line. Maybe eventually stranger boundaries we haven't defined yet.

He doesn't automatically know who crossed it or why.

He knows:

> Something came through here that didn't come through correctly.



That's extremely Anubis.

And it fits SKYBLOOD's existing boundary architecture.

This could eventually explain why RJ notices the false Elias's movement so quickly—but again, don't reveal that immediately.

Third ability—later, not now: PASSAGE

This should be his serious power.

RJ can allow something that legitimately belongs on the other side of a boundary to pass.

Not teleportation.

Not phasing through every wall.

Not portals.

Think more conceptually:

A boundary says NO.

RJ can sometimes determine:

This belongs through.

And the boundary accepts that judgment.

That makes him almost the inverse of Paper.

Paper:

> FILE SAYS NO.



RJ:

> It belongs here.



But there needs to be a hard restriction:

RJ cannot grant passage to himself merely because he wants something.

Otherwise he becomes a walking skeleton key.

His Anubis protection

Eventually, I would give him one defensive ability:

Things crossing a death-boundary incorrectly have difficulty touching RJ.

Notice the wording.

Not:

RJ is invulnerable.

A car can hit him.

A punch can hurt him.

A bullet is dangerous.

He's human.

But something that should be dead, should have passed, or is occupying a state it doesn't properly belong in has trouble imposing itself on him.

That's where the Anubis connection becomes formidable later.

And it potentially makes RJ extremely important to Abel / Thomas without making him stronger than Elias.

What I would absolutely NOT give him

No laser eyes. No gold energy. No super-strength matching Elias. No flying. No generic necromancy. No summoning armies of dead people. No jackal transformation yet. No black-and-gold superhero costume. No magical staff. No constant conversations with ghosts. No automatic truth detection.

And especially:

no gold eyes.

SB-CB01 still owns that.

If Anubis eventually produces a visual manifestation, it needs its own later canon lock rather than stealing recognition-gold.

The power relationship becomes excellent

Character	Primary domain

Elias	Intervention — I can do something.
RJ	Discernment — I can tell what belongs.
Paper	Classification — The record determines what counts.
FIRST SKY	Pressure/recognition — Contradiction will not remain contained.
Scale	Weighing — Both truths must carry their actual weight.


Now RJ isn't an add-on.

He occupies an empty position in the mythology.

And here's the part I like most:

Eventually somebody asks RJ:

> How do you know that's not Elias?



RJ looks at Elias.

Then the duplicate record.

> RJ:
Because Uncle E heavy.



It sounds like a kid making a joke.

Much later, the reader realizes:

RJ meant it literally.

Not pounds.

Weight.

My recommendation

Lock only the power architecture, not all four abilities yet:

> RJ / ANUBIS POWER LAW: RJ's supernatural domain concerns weight, rightful passage, and boundaries between states. His abilities reveal or adjudicate belonging; they do not duplicate Elias's physical powers. His natural observational intelligence remains real and useful independently of supernatural ability.



Then introduce True Weight first.

Let readers spend several issues wondering:

Is RJ just really good at noticing people?

Before we prove:

No. That boy can weigh something the rest of them can't see.

That would make his Anubis connection feel earned instead of turning him into a new powered character overnight.
