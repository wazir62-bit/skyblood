OPEN

MOTHER

Beat.

MOTHER

MOTHER
People.

MOTHER
Things about people.

Things it shouldn't know.

Never tell it who matters to you.

Elias thinks immediately of W.

The thing used Elias's face to reach him.

It wasn't simply copying Elias.

It was learning him.

GRANDFATHER'S FOUR RULES

ONE

Don't answer three knocks after the first thunder.

TWO

Don't follow somebody familiar if they don't call your name.

THREE

When the wind goes completely still, get under something.

FOUR

If it comes wearing you, don't let it see what you'll die for.

ELIAS
Why was Granddaddy telling you this?

MOTHER
He wasn't.

Beat.

MOTHER
He was telling you.

Elias absorbs that.

ELIAS

MOTHER
I don't know what he knew.

Elias's phone sounds.

Once.

Twice.

Three times.

W.

CALL ME.

NOW.

Third message:

Smoke rising above Memphis.

THE THREAT

Industrial fire.

Real workers.

Real danger.

The facility belongs to the same operating network connected to the dead address.

Elias calls W.

W is near the scene.

ELIAS
Go home.

Pause.

W
Which one?

Elias has no answer.

W
Turn the news on.

Elias does.

Fire.

Workers evacuating.

Emergency crews.

At the edge of the live shot stands a man who looks exactly like Elias.

Elias is standing beside his mother.

She looks from the television—

to her son—

then back.

MOTHER
Don't go.

ELIAS
People in there.

MOTHER
That's why it's there.

Grandfather's fourth rule:

Don't let it see what you'll die for.

ELIAS
Maybe that's the problem.

MOTHER
What?

ELIAS
It thinks it knows.

He leaves.

ONE OBJECTIVE

GET THE MISSING WORKERS OUT.

Everything serves that story.

---

THE HEADCOUNT

At the emergency perimeter, W is waiting.

ELIAS
You need to leave.

W
I'm staying until you do.

ELIAS
W—

W
I said staying. I ain't running inside no burning building.

Good.

W has grown too.

He can stand with Elias without behaving recklessly.

Emergency crews conduct headcounts.

Elias hears two totals.

Something doesn't reconcile.

He knows crews.

He knows shifts.

He knows how people disappear inside bad numbers.

ELIAS
Where's the other three?

They check.

Three workers are missing.

Elias didn't discover that because he's supernatural.

He discovered it because Elias Marrow knows his world.

---

ELIAS HELPS BEFORE BECOMING THE RESCUER

Responders correctly keep Elias outside.

He gives them:

facility knowledge,

worker locations,

alternate access,

and crew information.

They use it.

Then Elias feels the atmospheric pressure change.

He watches the smoke.

ELIAS
Move those people.

RESPONDER
Why?

ELIAS
Wind's about to turn.

The responder checks.

Nothing yet.

ELIAS
Please.

They reposition.

Seconds later—

the wind changes.

Smoke consumes the place where they had been standing.

W looks at Elias.

W
You knew.

Elias doesn't answer.

Because this time there is no comfortable denial.

---

THE THIRD WORKER

Two missing workers become reachable through an alternate service route Elias identified.

The third is isolated.

Alive.

Injured.

Elias knows another way through the facility.

Responders are trying to reach him but don't understand the route Elias is describing.

Elias looks toward the building.

W sees the decision.

W
Don't.

ELIAS
That's usually my line.

W
Then listen to yourself.

Beat.

ELIAS
Stay here.

W
Bring your ass back.

Elias goes.

W stays.

INSIDE

Smoke.

Heat.

Metal.

Sprinklers.

No superhero spectacle.

Elias finds the worker.

Gets his arm across his shoulder.

Starts moving.

Then—

the wind disappears.

Grandfather's third rule:

When the wind goes completely still, get under something.

Elias pulls the worker beneath reinforced structure.

A suspended component collapses where they had been.

Grandfather's rule just saved two lives.

Elias looks at the wreckage.

A real answer:

Grandfather knew something.

FALSE ELIAS

ELIAS

The entity has watched Elias protect W.

His mother.

His people.

It believes attachment is the mechanism controlling him.

But this man is nobody Elias knows.

And Elias still won't leave him.

The double looks almost curious.

FALSE ELIAS

ELIAS
Then help or move.

Then steps between them.

That's all.

Heavy.

Real.

Again.

THE ACTUAL CLIMAX

Elias now has what he has wanted all season:

A chance to follow something that might possess answers.

The double is right there.

For the first time Elias could pursue it.

Behind him—

the injured stranger struggles for air.

Elias looks toward the disappearing double.

Then toward the man.

One beat.

No speech.

Elias turns his back on the mystery.

Lifts the stranger.

Takes the newly cleared passage.

That is the climax.

Not beating the double.

Choosing the human being over the answer.

Three survivors.

Elias did not solve the mystery.

He saved lives.

That's his win.

SEASON ONE CORE

Threat: Something has begun studying Elias through the people around him.

Reaction: Elias tries to understand what's happening.

Action: He stops merely reacting and begins choosing what he'll do about it.

Climax: The entity assumes Elias's attachments control his heroism.

Answer: Elias chooses a complete stranger.

Victory: Three people live.

Transformation: The hero exists before the costume, name or mastered powers exist.

HIDDEN CANON

Throughout Issue #13:

closed geometry begins opening;

thresholds are crossed;

paired structures are left behind;

three remains present without becoming an obvious code;

the FIRST SKY diagram remains visually independent from real-world traditions;

the thirteen Season 1 fragments can still assemble into a larger structure;

one fragment still appears “wrong.”

And Grandfather's sentence remains:

THEY WILL COME BACK FOR THE BLOOD.

Readers will focus on THEY.

The long-game clue remains:

BACK.

Something is not arriving.

Something is returning.
